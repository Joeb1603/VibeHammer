# Force Disposition and the Mission Matrix

Sources: https://gdmissions.app/11th/force-disposition, https://gdmissions.app/11th/matrix

Each player picks a force disposition (their detachments decide which are available, see [detachments](detachments.md)). Your primary mission is the card in your disposition's deck that matches your opponent's disposition. The five force disposition cards on the site are images of exactly these rows.
