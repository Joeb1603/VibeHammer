# Primary Missions: Reconnaissance deck

Source: https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/reconnaissance

The Reconnaissance force disposition scores one of these five missions, chosen by the opponent's force disposition (see [the mission matrix](../force_disposition.md)).

Flags column: `or` = alternative to the row above, `cumulative` = adds to the rows above, `perUnit` = scored per unit, `eob` = end-of-battle scoring.

## Gather Intel

**You:** Reconnaissance · **Opponent:** Reconnaissance · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/reconnaissance/gather-intel) · [layouts](../layouts.md#reconnaissance-mirror)

### FIRST BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **central objectives**. | 6 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| For each friendly unit that completed the **Extract Intelligence action** this turn. | 7 | perUnit |

### END OF BATTLE

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| Three or more of your **operation markers** are on the battlefield. | 5 | eob |
| One of your **operation markers** is within range of your opponent's **home objective**. | 5 | eob |

### Action: Extract Intelligence

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase, from the second battle round onwards. |
| **UNITS** | One unit within range of one **objective** (excluding your **home objective**) that does not have any of your **operation markers** within range of it. |
| **USE LIMIT** | Unlimited. Each unit that starts this action this phase must be within range of a different **objective**. |
| **COMPLETES** | End of your turn, if your unit controls that **objective**. |
| **EFFECT** | Place one of your **operation markers** within range of that **objective**. |

## Surveil the Foe

**You:** Reconnaissance · **Opponent:** Disruption · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/reconnaissance/surveil-the-foe) · [layouts](../layouts.md#reconnaissance-vs-disruption)

Each time a friendly unit ends a move within range of one **objective** that has any of your **opponent's operation** markers within range of it, remove those **operation markers** from the battlefield.

### ANY BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| One or more enemy units were **surveilled** this turn (see reverse), unless each of those units is within range of one or more **objectives** that have one or more **operation markers** within range of them. | 4 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |
| You control more **objectives** than your opponent. | 4 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| None of your opponent's **operation markers** are on the battlefield. | 5 |  |

### Action: Surveil the Foe

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase. |
| **UNITS** | One friendly unit. |
| **USE LIMIT** | Unlimited. |
| **COMPLETES** | Immediately. |
| **EFFECT** | Select one enemy unit within 18" of and visible to your unit that has not been **surveilled** this turn: until the end of the turn, that enemy unit is **surveilled**. |

## Triangulation

**You:** Reconnaissance · **Opponent:** Purge the Foe · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/reconnaissance/triangulation) · [layouts](../layouts.md#reconnaissance-vs-purge-the-foe)

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| One **objective** is **triangulated** (see reverse). | 3 |  |
| Two **objectives** are **triangulated**. | 6 | or |
| Three or more **objectives** are **triangulated**. | 10 | or |

### END OF BATTLE

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control four or more **objectives**. | 10 | eob |

### Action: Triangulate

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase, from the second battle round onwards. |
| **UNITS** | One friendly unit within range of one **objective** (excluding your **home objective**). |
| **USE LIMIT** | Once per turn. |
| **COMPLETES** | End of your turn, if your unit controls that **objective**. |
| **EFFECT** | That **objective** is **triangulated**: place one of your **operation markers** within range of that **objective**. |
