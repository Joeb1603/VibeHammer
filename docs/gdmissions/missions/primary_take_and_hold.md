# Primary Missions: Take and Hold deck

Source: https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/take-and-hold

The Take and Hold force disposition scores one of these five missions, chosen by the opponent's force disposition (see [the mission matrix](../force_disposition.md)).

Flags column: `or` = alternative to the row above, `cumulative` = adds to the rows above, `perUnit` = scored per unit, `eob` = end-of-battle scoring.

## Battlefield Dominance

**You:** Take and Hold · **Opponent:** Take and Hold · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/take-and-hold/battlefield-dominance) · [layouts](../layouts.md#take-and-hold-mirror)

### FIRST & SECOND BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control more **objectives** than your opponent. | 2 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| For each **objective** you control. | 3 | perUnit |
| For each of those **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**) if you control your **home objective**. | 2 | cumulative, perUnit |
