# Primary Missions: Purge the Foe deck

Source: https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/purge-the-foe

The Purge the Foe force disposition scores one of these five missions, chosen by the opponent's force disposition (see [the mission matrix](../force_disposition.md)).

Flags column: `or` = alternative to the row above, `cumulative` = adds to the rows above, `perUnit` = scored per unit, `eob` = end-of-battle scoring.

## Consecrate

**You:** Purge the Foe · **Opponent:** Reconnaissance · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/purge-the-foe/consecrate) · [layouts](../layouts.md#purge-the-foe-vs-reconnaissance)

Each time a friendly unit **destroys** a unit, that friendly unit becomes a **consecration** unit. At the end of your turn, for each of your **consecration** units, you can select one **objective** it is within range of (excluding your **home objective**) that has not been **consecrated**. If you do, place one of your operation markers within range of that **objective** – that **objective** is **consecrated** and that unit is no longer a **consecration** unit.

### ANY BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| One or two **objectives** are **consecrated**. | 3 |  |
| Three or more **objectives** are **consecrated**. | 6 | or |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |
| You control more **objectives** than your opponent. | 4 |  |

### END OF BATTLE

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| Your opponent's **home objective** is **consecrated**. | 5 | eob |

## Punishment

**You:** Purge the Foe · **Opponent:** Disruption · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/purge-the-foe/punishment) · [layouts](../layouts.md#purge-the-foe-vs-disruption)

**START OF YOUR TURN:** Select one to three enemy units that are on the battlefield and within range of **objectives** and/or that **destroyed** one or more friendly units in the previous turn.
