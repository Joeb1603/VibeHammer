# Primary Missions: Disruption deck

Source: https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/disruption

The Disruption force disposition scores one of these five missions, chosen by the opponent's force disposition (see [the mission matrix](../force_disposition.md)).

Flags column: `or` = alternative to the row above, `cumulative` = adds to the rows above, `perUnit` = scored per unit, `eob` = end-of-battle scoring.

## Death Trap

**You:** Disruption · **Opponent:** Take and Hold · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/disruption/death-trap) · [layouts](../layouts.md#disruption-vs-take-and-hold)

### ANY BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| For each **terrain area trapped** this turn. | 2 | perUnit |
| For each of those **terrain areas** that is an **objective**. | 3 | cumulative, perUnit |

### ANY BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| One or more enemy units that started the turn within a terrain area were destroyed, if that terrain area is **trapped**. | 3 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |

### Action: Booby Trap

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase. |
| **UNITS** | One friendly unit within range of one **objective** (excluding your **home objective**) or within one **terrain area** that is not within your deployment zone, that you have not yet **trapped** (see below). |
| **USE LIMIT** | Unlimited. Each unit that starts this **action** this phase must be within a different **terrain area**. |
| **COMPLETES** | Immediately. |
| **EFFECT** | That **terrain area** is **trapped**: place one of your **operation markers** within that **terrain area**. |

## Locate and Deny

**You:** Disruption · **Opponent:** Priority Assets · [card page](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/disruption/locate-and-deny) · [layouts](../layouts.md#disruption-vs-priority-assets)

**START OF THE BATTLE:** Select five **terrain areas** not within your deployment zone; for each one, place one of your **operation markers** within it. If you cannot, do so for each **terrain area** that is not within your deployment zone.

### ANY BATTLE ROUND

**When:** End of your turn

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| One or more enemy units that started the turn within range of one or more **objectives** are **destroyed**. | 4 |  |
| Only one of your **operation markers** is on the battlefield, if one or more of your units are within the same **terrain area** as that **marker**, and no enemy units are within that **terrain area**. | 4 |  |

### SECOND BATTLE ROUND ONWARDS

**When:** End of your Command phase (or the end of your turn in the fifth battle round)

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| You control one or more **objectives** (excluding your **home objective**). | 4 |  |

### END OF BATTLE

| Condition | VP | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| Only one of your **operation markers** is on the battlefield, if one or more of your units are within the same **terrain area** as that **marker**, and no enemy units are within that **terrain area**. | 5 | eob |

### Action: Sensor Sweep

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase. |
| **UNITS** | One friendly unit within range of one **central objective**. |
| **USE LIMIT** | Once per turn. |
| **COMPLETES** | End of your turn, if your unit controls that **objective**. |
| **EFFECT** | Your unit **performs a sensor sweep**: remove one **operation marker** from the battlefield. |
| **RESTRICTION** | A unit cannot start this **action** if there is only one **operation marker** on the battlefield. |

### Action: Decoy

| | |
|---|---|
| **STARTS** | Your Shooting phase. |
| **UNITS** | One friendly unit within range of one **objective** (excluding your **home objective**) that is not **decoyed** (see below). |
| **USE LIMIT** | Unlimited. Each unit that starts this **action** this phase must be within range of a different **objective**. |
| **COMPLETES** | End of your turn, if your unit controls that **objective**. |
| **EFFECT** | That **objective** is **decoyed**: place one of your **operation markers** within range of that **objective**. |
