## D-097: The opponent-turn core stratagems: Fire Overwatch with Snap Shooting (15.08, 15.09), Heroic Intervention (15.11), Counteroffensive (15.12); objective control first at a phase's end (14.02.01); format-4 replays (M96; amends D-020, D-084, D-093, D-095, D-096)
**Decided:** 2026-11-26 (M96; the user's rulings 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-26; the design reviewed in plan mode). Written
before the code (design first); the results are added at the end.

### 1. The user's rulings
- **[TORRENT] when snap shooting** (2026-11-25): it still hits automatically (24.37); no hit roll is made.
- **A snap shot's 6 is not a critical hit** (2026-11-26). The app's 15.09 (data v963, 2026-09-30) adds "Those hits are
  not critical hits"; the PDF says nothing, so by 05.01's table it would be. The app's later text is followed, so
  [SUSTAINED HITS] adds nothing to a snap shot (FAQ Q55 and Q56: only a 6 hits).
- **When the windows open** (2026-11-26): Fire Overwatch and Heroic Intervention are offered only when they can do
  something. Fire Overwatch needs a unit with a valid snap target; Heroic Intervention needs a unit and mode with a
  possible charge target (Leap to Defend: an enemy unit within 12" that made a charge move this phase; Into the Fray: an
  enemy unit within 6"). This narrows D-096's "no usefulness judged" to "never offered when the effect can do nothing
  whatever the dice".
- **No re-target when snap shooting** (2026-11-26): 15.09's "only one ... enemy unit" holds, so 04.03.03 (D-093) gives a
  snap shot no new target, and attacks left without one are lost.
- **Counteroffensive in the fight sequence** (2026-11-26):
  - used during Resolve Remaining Combats, play returns to Resolve Fights First Combats at once (12.04's return check
    sees the new Fights First unit);
  - its player may still pass (D-020's PassFight), and "must be the next unit you select" lapses if the unit can't be
    selected;
  - the window opens after any enemy unit has fought, with attacks or without (FAQ Q2).

### 2. Readings settled by the text
- 01.07 (FAQ Q50): none of the three can target a battle-shocked unit.
- Heroic Intervention's unit must be eligible to declare a charge (11.02, FAQ Q71). In the opponent's turn the advance
  and fall-back conditions can't fail, but the check (`declare_charge_problem`) is made.
- 07.02.01 Out of Phase Rules (app): during Fire Overwatch, rules locked to "your Shooting phase" don't apply: [HEAVY]
  (24.16) and 17.03 (an engaged MONSTER or VEHICLE can be shot) are off. The check keys on `ShootingType::Snap`, not
  on the stage, because the bot and the client compute hypothetical shots from other stages.
- FAQ Q45: Into the Fray's cost is 2CP outright, not a modifier.
- FAQ Q62: an effect that lasts "until the end of the phase/turn" still applies in that end step.
- Counteroffensive's WHEN is the Fight step, so it never follows a forced fight (the Consolidate step's).
- 15.01: a unit targeted by one stratagem can't be targeted by another in the same phase. So Command Re-roll can't
  re-roll Heroic Intervention's charge roll or the Fire Overwatch and Counteroffensive units' rolls; M95's records
  already enforce it. 15.09 forbids hit re-rolls too, which gets its own refusal.
- 15.09 AFTER SHOOTING ("not eligible to start an action"): the engine has no actions yet (`TODO(actions)`).

### 3. The windows (amends D-096)
- `WindowTiming` gains `EndOfPhase` (15.08, 15.11) and `AfterAttacks` (15.12). A window that follows no roll has no
  roll; an EndOfPhase window has no unit; an AfterAttacks window's unit is the enemy unit that has just fought.
  `decision::UseStratagems::unit` and `StratagemWindow::{roll, unit}` become optional, and M95's windows serialize and
  hash as before.
- `action::UseStratagem` gains `mode` (Heroic Intervention's), and for the new timings its unit is the target the player
  picks. `WindowInfo`'s options carry their mode, their use cost and the units they can target.
- **Where they open:**
  - entering each End of Phase stage, after objective control (section 7): Fire Overwatch at the end of the Movement
    phase and Heroic Intervention at the end of the Charge phase, for the player whose turn it isn't (the turn player's
    own end-of-phase rules come first by 01.03.02; none exist yet);
  - just after a unit has fought in the Fight step (after D-020's update, its [HAZARDOUS] rolls and their mortal wounds
    included): Counteroffensive, for the other player;
  - never after the game has ended (D-007).
- The closure holds: `apply_action` declines every window inline.

### 4. Fire Overwatch (15.08, 1CP) and Snap Shooting (15.09)
- **Target:** an unengaged unit, not TITANIC, that can shoot (`unit_shoot_problem`) and has a snap shot option.
- **Effect:** the unit shoots using snap shooting. A `SnapShoot{unit}` decision follows, answered by
  `Shoot{unit, Snap, shots}`. While the unit shoots its player is the active player (01.03).
- **Snap shooting:** a new `ShootingType::Snap`, never in 10.02's list.
  - Every shot is at one target unit within 24" of the unit (unit to unit, D-009), visible and eligible (04.01, 04.02;
    each weapon's own range still applies per model).
  - `judge_hit_rolls`: an unmodified 6 hits and is not critical, every other result fails, whatever the BS and the
    modifiers; [TORRENT] hits automatically.
  - No Command Re-roll window after a snap hit roll (15.09).
  - [RAPID FIRE], [BLAST], [HAZARDOUS] and 24.07 apply as usual; [HEAVY] and 17.03 don't (section 2).
  - No re-target (section 1).

### 5. Heroic Intervention (15.11, 1CP; Into the Fray 2CP)
- **Target:** an unengaged unit within 12" of an enemy unit, eligible to declare a charge (11.02), a VEHICLE only if it
  is also a CHARACTER or WALKER, with a possible target for the chosen mode (section 1).
- **Effect:** resolve a charge (11.02). The mode is chosen with the stratagem, before the charge roll.
  - The charge is declared (`ChargeDeclared`); the charge roll is the unit's player's `RollDice{Charge}`.
  - Into the Fray: a total above 6 becomes 6 (`ChargeRolled::capped`).
  - Charge targets (11.04 BEFORE MOVING), filtered by the mode: Leap to Defend, enemy units that made a charge move
    this phase; Into the Fray, enemy units within 6". Both within 12" and within the maximum distance.
  - The charge move is 11.04's; it gives Fights First until the end of the turn. A failed charge moves nothing (D-018).

### 6. Counteroffensive (15.12, 2CP; amends D-020)
- **Target:** a unit of the player's that is eligible to fight (12.04).
- **Effect:** until the end of the phase the unit has Fights First (`UnitPhaseFlags::fights_first`), and it must be the
  next unit its player selects to fight (`FightSequence::next_to_fight`). D-020 makes the opponent of the player who just
  fought select next, so the Counteroffensive player always selects next.
  - In Resolve Remaining Combats, play returns to Resolve Fights First Combats at once.
  - PassFight stays allowed; the requirement lapses if the unit can't be selected.
  - The flag is cleared on leaving the End of Fight phase step.

### 7. 14.02.01: objective control first (amends D-084, D-093)
At the end of each phase and turn, objective control is determined first (app 14.02.01). It moves from leaving each End
of Phase stage to entering it, before any window. Before M96 nothing happened between the two, so every event and
state comes out the same; with Fire Overwatch, its kills change control only at the end of the Shooting phase.

### 8. Format 4 (amends D-014, D-025, D-095, D-096)
- Format 4 records the answers to the new windows. A format-3 file can't hold them, and an M96 window can come just
  before an M95 one, so the format changes (as M94 and M95 did).
- **Format 3 → 4:** before each recorded step and at the end, every pending EndOfPhase or AfterAttacks window is
  declined; the recorded steps answer M95's windows. Formats 1 and 2 convert as in M95 (every window declined).
- `with_roll_steps` returns format 4; `run_replay`, the replay viewer, saves and `M88_Replay` convert formats 1 to 3;
  `Match::to_replay` and saves write 4.
- All replays are regenerated as format 4, checked against the previous files: the same initial state, `expect` and
  steps once the declines are taken out, and only declines added. The M95 and M96 event dumps are byte-identical with no
  filter (no new event in a game without the new stratagems).

### 9. The client and the bot
- **Client:**
  - the window asks for the unit (on the board or from a list) and, for Heroic Intervention, the mode with its cost;
  - snap shooting runs through the shot editor with its limits;
  - the cards and the log say what each stratagem did (a snap hit card needs a 6; a capped charge roll shows the cap).
- **Bot:**
  - keys `fire_overwatch`, `heroic_intervention` and `counteroffensive` (0 or 1), with heuristics in
    `normal/stratagems.cpp`;
  - the session declines a window that offers only stratagems a side doesn't play, so a bot never sees one;
  - the defaults are set by D-092's keep rule, with the identity proof at `command_reroll=0` and at the defaults.

### 10. Results

  gains no event, and 14.02.01's move (control determined on entering the End of Phase step) changes no event's order.
  `D097_Closure` plays random games with the new windows declined paced and the same choices inline: the same events
  and states.
- *Replays:* all 30 regenerated as format 4. `D097_Replays.DISABLED_CompareWithFormat3Files` checked each against its
  format-3 file: each new file is exactly the old one converted by `with_roll_steps` (the same description, initial
  state and `expect`), with only the new windows' declines added (129).
  - New `m96_reactions.json` (seed 1): Fire Overwatch (a snap 6 hits), the Intercessors' charge, Heroic Intervention
    with Into the Fray (a roll above 6 capped at 6 that still reaches), and Counteroffensive, whose Termagants fight

  - The legacy fixtures: `m8_shooting` and `m62_random_characteristics` as format 3 convert to the current files; the
    three M95 saves load as the same games played now (one still waiting at its Command Re-roll window, one with Side
    B's Fire Overwatch window declined after its Movement phase).
- *Tests:* `R15_08_*` (8), `R15_09_*` (8), `R15_11_*` (8), `R15_12_*` (8), `R14_02_01_*` (2), `D097_*` (5, and three
  disabled tools: the seed search, the regeneration and the format check), client `M96_*` (12) and bot `M96_*` (7). Random play from V1, the rosters and a melee start uses each
  new stratagem and keeps the invariants, each game replaying as format 4.
- *Refusals:* each stratagem's WHEN, 15.01's cost (Into the Fray's 2CP named in it), once per phase and the unit already

- *As built, beside the design:*

    (1 CP): Termagants", "Into the Fray (2 CP): Termagants 2"); there is no board mode with range rings.
  - Counteroffensive's lapse (its unit can't be selected) can't happen in this game but by a pass: the unit is the
    next selection at once, and a fight type is always open to it. The code keeps the check; the pass is tested.
  - A forced fight (12.08) is the Consolidate step's, so its window check (the Fight step only) is by construction;
    no test reaches a forced fight of the turn player's unit.
  - `core::kAllStratagems` lists the implemented stratagems for the client and the bots.
  - The arena's and the client's sessions decline per stratagem from the bot's keys (`BotConfig::declined_stratagems`).
- *Client:* xvfb screenshots of each window (Fire Overwatch, Heroic Intervention with both modes, Counteroffensive),
  the snap shot's editor and its hit card ("Need 6 to hit (snap shooting, 15.09)", the 6s green, not gold). The bot
  smoke test (`--bot both --bot-steps 5`) takes its 5 actions.
- *Bots:*
  - Three keys, 0 or 1, in `normal/stratagems.cpp` (values in unit shares, value.hpp):
    - Fire Overwatch: the unit whose best snap shot (`plan_snap_shooting`: one target, every model's loadouts at it,
      improved as `plan_shooting`; the odds key gains the snap flag, so only 6s hit and none is critical) is worth
      the most, used at 0.05 or more.
    - Heroic Intervention: the unit and mode with the most gain over holding per CP, E[max(charged, hold)] - hold over

      the engine applies the mode's targets and Into the Fray's cap), used at 0.05 a CP or more; a tie goes to Leap to
      Defend.
    - Counteroffensive: only when it changes who fights next (a Fights First step in which the enemy has an eligible

      sequence's state, not on a scratch Simulator.
    - The snap shot's decision is the bot's plan; the fallback fills a one-target snap shot.
  - The session declines a window that offers the bot only stratagems whose key is 0, so the default bot never sees
    the new windows, nor Insane Bravery's.
  - *Identity at `command_reroll=0`:* all 13 runs, 200 games each (2,600 games), are identical to the M95 binary in

  - *Identity at the defaults:* every per-game field but `steps`, `actions` and `trace` is identical to the M95 binary
