## D-089: Benefit of cover and worsening BS (13.08; "Modified Characteristics", app-only rule)
**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (M88's plan mode; design approved by the user, who chose the working assumption below for the
app-only part). **Implemented:** M88 (pending). Uses D-087's areas and features and D-088's Fully Visible.

- **13.08 Benefit of cover.** Decided per attacking model and target unit when the targets are selected (04.02 Select
  Targets), the same pattern as [RAPID FIRE] (24.30), [BLAST] (24.05) and 17.03, and recorded on the selection
  (`WeaponSelection::benefit_of_cover`, JSON omitted when false). The target has the benefit of cover against that
  model's attacks if every model in the target unit meets one or more of:
  - it has the INFANTRY, BEASTS or SWARM keyword and is within a terrain area (any category, Exposed included);
  - it is not fully visible to the attacking model due to intervening terrain (D-088).

  Models the target loses while the shooting is resolved do not change it: the attacks targeted the unit as selected.
- **Worsening BS: the working assumption.** "Modified Characteristics" and "Characteristic Modifiers" are app-only
  rules (05 and 02 "See also"); their wording is not in `docs/rules/`. Like D-009, this reading is not verified against
  the app; revisit it if the official text differs.
  - "Worsen the BS characteristic of that attack by 1" raises the attack's BS number by 1: 3+ becomes 4+.
  - Once per attack: the benefit of cover is had or not, so sources don't stack.
  - It changes the characteristic, not the hit roll, so D-011's cap on hit-roll modifiers does not apply to it. Hit-roll
    modifiers ([HEAVY] +1, 10.06 and 17.03 −1) still apply to the roll, capped as before.
  - 05.01's table is unchanged: an unmodified 1 fails and an unmodified 6 is a critical hit, whatever the BS. A BS
    worsened to 7+ hits only with an unmodified 6, or a modified 7 or more.
  - [TORRENT] attacks have no hit roll (24.37) and are unaffected.
- **Where it is applied.** When the attack profile is built from the selection (`attacks.cpp`, beside the hit modifier):
  the profile's skill is worsened. So D-006 keeps attacks with and without cover in separate batches (their profiles
  differ), and the client's dice card shows the BS that applies.
- **Stealth (24.33)** gives the benefit of cover too; it is a unit ability and is left out of M88 by the user's choice
  (PLAN.md, Later).
