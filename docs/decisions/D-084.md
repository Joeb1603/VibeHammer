## D-084: The objective mission: objectives first, kills still count (M86; amends D-076 for scenarios that ask for it)
**Decided:** 2026-11-11 (agreed with the user while planning M85–M87; the numbers were left to Claude to balance).
**Implemented:** M86. **Tuned:** M87, in the scenario file only.

07.03 leaves what is scored to the mission, and the core rules have no mission, so this is a house mission. A scenario
with `"result": "mission"` uses it. Every number below lives in the scenario file, so balancing never needs code.

- **Areas (14.02).** One 12"-diameter objective area at the centre of the battlefield; one 6"-diameter home area per
  side, centred on the centre line 6" from that side's own edge (inside its deployment zone). They are terrain
  objectives with no terrain effects until terrain exists (Later, item 1).
- **Objective VP.** In each player's own Command phase in battle rounds 2–5, that player scores for each area they
  control: **middle 5 VP, own home 2 VP, enemy home 4 VP.** Scoring then, after the opponent's turn, means an objective
  scores only if it was held through that turn. Each player has 4 scorings. Raiding the enemy home pays, which gives a
  second fight away from the middle.
- **Kill VP.** At the end of the battle, **1 VP per full 50 points destroyed**, using D-076's points destroyed (partial
  units count by wounds lost), rounded down in D-076's exact integer arithmetic.
- **Result.** A wipe-out still wins at once (D-007). Otherwise the most VP after battle round 5 wins; equal VP is
  decided by points destroyed (D-076); equal again is a draw.
- **Why these numbers.** Objectives can give up to 4 x (5 + 2 + 4) = 44 VP. A typical game might hold the middle half
  the time and the home throughout (about 18 VP), against about 8 VP for destroying 40% of a 1,000-point army. So
  objectives decide most games, while kills still swing a close one, and destroying units is how objectives are won.
  This is a starting point: M87 measures the balance with the arena and tunes the numbers.
