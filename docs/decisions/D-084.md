## D-084: The objective mission: objectives first, kills still count (M86; amends D-076 for scenarios that ask for it)
**Decided:** 2026-11-11 (agreed with the user while planning M85–M87; the numbers were left to Claude to balance).
**Implemented:** M86. **Tuned:** M87, in the scenario file only (below: Centre 1 VP, homes 4 / 8, 1 kill VP per 25
points; the user's choice, 2026-11-12).

07.03 leaves what is scored to the mission, and the core rules have no mission, so this is a house mission. A scenario
with `"result": "mission"` uses it. Every number below lives in the scenario file, so balancing never needs code.

- **Areas (14.02).** One 12"-diameter objective area at the centre of the battlefield; one 6"-diameter home area per
  side, centred on the centre line 6" from that side's own edge (inside its deployment zone). They are terrain
  objectives with no terrain effects until terrain exists (Later, item 1).
- **Objective VP.** In each player's own Command phase in battle rounds 2–5, that player scores for each area they
  control: **middle 5 VP, own home 2 VP, enemy home 4 VP.** Scoring then, after the opponent's turn, means an objective
  scores only if it was held through that turn. Each player has 4 scorings. Raiding the enemy home pays, which gives a
  second fight away from the middle.
- **Kill VP.** At the end of the battle, **1 VP per full 50 points destroyed**, using D-076's points destroyed (partial
  units count by wounds lost), rounded down in D-076's exact integer arithmetic.
- **Result.** A wipe-out still wins at once (D-007). Otherwise the most VP after battle round 5 wins; equal VP is
  decided by points destroyed (D-076); equal again is a draw.
- **Why these numbers.** Objectives can give up to 4 x (5 + 2 + 4) = 44 VP. A typical game might hold the middle half
  the time and the home throughout (about 18 VP), against about 8 VP for destroying 40% of a 1,000-point army. So
  objectives decide most games, while kills still swing a close one, and destroying units is how objectives are won.
  This is a starting point: M87 measures the balance with the arena and tunes the numbers.

**Implemented (M86, 2026-11-12).** As agreed in M86's plan mode, with the choices below made for it.
- **Scenario file.** `"result": "mission"` with a `"mission"` block, in inches like the rest of the file:
  `{"first_scoring_round": 2, "kill_vp": {"points": 50, "vp": 1}, "objectives": [{"name", "x_in", "y_in",
  "diameter_in", "vp": {"A", "B"}}, ...]}`. Each area's VP are per side, so "own home 2, enemy home 4" is
  `{"A": 2, "B": 4}` on A's home. The block and the result go together. Every unit needs points. The loader and the
  state JSON refuse the rest as `DataError`:
  - a first scoring round outside 1..battle_rounds;
  - kill points outside 1..100000, or kill VP outside 0..1000;
  - fewer than 1 or more than 32 areas;
  - an area with an empty name, a diameter of 0 or less, or not wholly on the battlefield;
  - area VP outside 0..1000;
  - negative VP scored;
  - numbers that could take a total past 2^30.

  These bounds are far above any real mission, so no VP total can overflow.
- **`data/scenarios/incursion_objectives_44x60.json`.** `rosters_44x60.json`'s armies, formations and points, plus
  three areas: the Centre (22", 30"), 12", 5/5; A's home (22", 6"), 6", A 2 / B 4; B's home (22", 54"), 6", A 4 / B 2
  (south is y = 0). Both homes are controlled from the first check:
  - A's home by the Chaos Terminators' back rank (their two ranks reach 8.85" from the south edge);
  - B's home by the held-back Tyrant Guard and Tyrannofex.

  The start screen's default scenario is now this file. A saved settings file keeps the scenario it names.
- **State.** `ResultRule::Mission` (JSON `"result_rule": "mission"`) and `GameState::mission` (`Mission`: the numbers,
  each `ObjectiveArea` with its `controller` and `secured_by`, and `objective_vp` per side; JSON `"mission"`, omitted
  without one; an area's `controller` and `secured_by` are omitted when unset). Every older file loads and saves byte
  for byte. `GameResult` and `GameOver` carry a `VpTally` (objective, kill and total VP per side) when the game was
  decided on the mission.
- **Within range (14.01, 14.02, D-009).** Any part of the base within the area: the area is treated as a round base of
  its diameter, and the base-to-base distance must be at most 0 (EPS_MM), through the oval kernel. So an oval is
  measured by its own shape, and round-to-round is the old float code.
- **Level of control.** The sum of OC of the player's models within range. OC '-' adds nothing; this covers a
  battle-shocked unit (01.07), through the existing `objective_control(unit, model)`.
- **When control is determined (14.02).**
  - At the end of every phase: on leaving its End of Phase step, before `PhaseEnded`.
  - At the end of every turn: on leaving the End of Turn step, after Regaining Coherency's removals and before
    `TurnEnded`.

  An `ObjectiveControlChanged` event (14.02) is emitted only on a change. Between checks control holds whatever moves.
  A wipe-out ends the game without a check.
- **14.03 Secured objectives.** State only: nothing secures an objective yet. A secured area stays with its player on
  equal levels and with nobody there. It is lost only when the opponent's level is greater at the end of a **phase**;
  the end-of-turn check alone doesn't end it (the literal reading of "at the end of a phase"). Once the opponent gains
  control, the area is no longer secured.
- **"Controlling" units (14.02).** A query only (`controlling_units`), for M87: the controller's units within range
  that have a model with OC 1 or more.
- **Scoring.** On entering the Start of Command Phase step (08.01) in battle rounds `first_scoring_round`..last, the
  turn's player scores each area they control (`VictoryPointsScored`, D-084). An area worth 0 VP to them scores
  nothing and emits no event. Control changes only at phase and turn ends, so it is the control as the opponent's turn
  ended.
- **Kill VP.** `kill_vp` x floor(points destroyed / `kill_points`), from D-076's exact tally. The floor is taken as
  two integer divisions (numerator / denominator, then / kill_points), so D x kill_points is never formed.
- **Result.** A wipe-out still wins at once and cites D-007, with no VP in the result. Otherwise, after the last
  battle round:
  - the most VP wins: "D-084: battle round 5 has ended with both sides on the battlefield; victory points: Side A 28
    (28 objectives + 0 kills), Side B 8 (8 objectives + 0 kills); Side A wins on victory points";
  - equal VP adds "equal victory points (n each); points destroyed: Side A x, Side B y;" and then "Side A wins on
    points destroyed" or "equal points destroyed: the game is a draw".

  Such a `GameOver` cites D-084.
- **Custom battles.**
  - `core::custom_battle_scenario` takes an optional mission block. Without one it is as before, so the m73, m75 and
    m77 fixtures and replays are unchanged.
  - The client's army builder (`ArmyBuilder::load_mission`) and the arena's command line (`ArmySource::mission`) give
    it the objective scenario's block (`load_mission_block`). The tests' builders and army sources have none.
- **Client.**
  - *Board:* each area is drawn under the grid and the bases, filled with its controller's side colour (grey when
    nobody controls it), with a thicker outline when secured, and labelled above it ("Centre 5/5").
  - *Game panel:* "Victory points: Side A 9 (7 + 2 kills), Side B 2 (2 + 0 kills)", the kills being what the points
    destroyed give now. The points line stays.
  - *Victory pop-up:* both sides' VP, with objective and kill VP.
  - *Log lines:* "[14.02] Side A takes control of Centre (OC 4 to 2)" and "[D-084] Side B scores 2 VP for holding B
    home (6 objective VP)".
  - *Army builder:* names the mission.
- **Bot.**
  - It ignores the objectives (M87 plays for them). A mission counts as the points rule in its move score, because
    kills give VP and settle equal VP, so it plays the objective scenario exactly as the rosters: the same actions and
    trace lines, game for game (`M86_Bot`).
  - `state_hash` adds the mission behind a tag only when there is one.
  - The arena reports, for a mission scenario, the wins on VP and on points (equal VP), the draws, and the mean
    objective VP, kill VP and points destroyed per side.

**Tuned (M87, 2026-11-12; D-086).** With the bot playing for the objectives (D-086), the numbers above made the
objective scenario a Tyranid win nearly every game: they hold the Centre almost uncontested, with more and cheaper OC
(Termagants: OC 20 for 55 points; the armies' OC 67 against 56). Measured (Release arena, the bot on both sides, 100
seeds with each side going first; the Chaos Space Marines' share of the 200 games):

| Mission (homes 2 own / 4 raid, 1 kill VP per 50 points, unless named) | Chaos wins | objective / kill VP a game |
|---|---|---|
| Centre 5 (as decided above) | 2% | 31.1 / 17.8 |
| three middle areas (left, centre, right), 8", 2 VP each | 8.5% | 29.3 / 16.0 |
| Centre 3 | 5% | 24.7 / 17.4 |
| Centre 2 | 9.5% | 21.8 / 16.3 |
| Centre 1 | 25% | 18.6 / 15.5 |
| Centre 0 (homes only) | 57.5% | 15.6 / 14.9 |
| homes 4 / 8, 1 VP per 25 points, Centre 1 | 32.5% | 33.8 / 31.2 |
| homes 4 / 8, 1 VP per 25 points, Centre 2 | 23% | 37.2 / 32.0 |

Random armies stayed at 20–22% for the Chaos Space Marines whatever the mission (they favoured the Tyranids 3 to 1 on
points before any mission). No symmetric mission reached 40–60% with a Centre that counts, so the user chose the small
Centre: **Centre 1 VP; own home 4 VP, enemy home 8 VP; 1 kill VP per full 25 points destroyed**, the areas' sizes and
places and the first scoring round as above. With the kill rate doubled the homes and kills keep their M86 weight
against each other and the Centre counts half a VP of the old scale. Objective VP stay just above kill VP; the balance
(32.5%, short of 40%) is open (PLAN.md, M87). The scenario file and the custom battles' block carry the new numbers;
M86's numbers are kept as `tests/data/scenarios/m86_objectives_44x60.json` for the D-084 rule tests and the M86
replay.
