## D-087: Terrain in 2D: areas, features, categories, the scenario format, movement and set-up (13.01–13.06, 03.01)
**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (M88's plan mode; design approved by the user). The user chose a 2D footprint model (no floors,
vertical moves or Plunging Fire), a point-symmetric layout proposed by Claude with the three objective areas as real
terrain areas (14.01), and a dense ruin for the Centre. **Implemented:** M88 (pending).

**Why 2D.** Section 13 assumes real models with heights, floors and windows. The engine is top-down and `Position.z` is
always 0, so M88 keeps every rule that can be read on the battlefield plane and leaves the vertical ones (13.06 Moving
Vertically, upper surfaces, 22.05 Plunging Fire, 13.11 gaps above 3") for a later milestone with z (PLAN.md, Later).

- **Shapes.** A *terrain area* (13.01) is a convex polygon or a circle and holds 1 to 16 *terrain features*. A feature
  is a convex polygon or a circle lying wholly within its area (13.01: "wholly within that boundary"). Polygons have 3
  to 16 vertices and a positive area; they are stored counter-clockwise. Non-convex pieces (an L-shaped ruin) are two
  features.
- **Categories (13.02).** Each feature is Exposed, Light or Dense. A Dense feature has a height (inches in the file),
  the only height M88 reads (13.06's 2"). Areas in one battlefield may mix categories (13.05).
- **Exposed features** (craters, scattered debris) are at ground level: models move through them and can be set up or
  end a move on them. They block no movement and no line of sight.
- **Light and Dense features** are solid footprints (walls, barricades, containers). With no upper surfaces in M88:
  - no model may be set up or end a move with its base overlapping one (03.01 Ending a Move: "partway through a surface
    of a terrain feature"; 13.06 Setting Up or Ending a Move: ground level only, since nothing stands on top in M88).
    Overlap is D-027's rule: by more than EPS_MM; base contact is allowed;
  - setting up includes the scenario's formations (the loader refuses them) and D-081's hand deployment (its scan skips
    such spots).
- **13.06 Dense movement.** INFANTRY, BEASTS, SWARM and MOBILE models move horizontally through Dense features. Any
  other model's path, each straight line and (D-074) each turn of an oval, may not cross a Dense feature more than 2"
  tall; it crosses lower ones. Moving vertically to ascend such sections is not offered in M88 (`TODO(z)`).
  Exposed and Light features never block movement (13.06: all models move through them).
- **Engagement range and distances** ignore terrain (03.04 is horizontal; no z).
- **Terrain objectives (14.01).** An area with `"objective": "<name>"` takes that mission objective's circle as its
  shape, so "that terrain area is the objective" holds by construction. Objective control (D-084) is unchanged.
- **Scenario format.** A `"terrain"` block, in inches like the rest of the file:
  ```json
  "terrain": {"areas": [
    {"name": "Centre", "objective": "Centre", "features": [
      {"name": "Ruin wall", "category": "dense", "height_in": 5, "polygon_in": [[17,27],[23,27],[23,27.5],[17,27.5]]}]},
    {"name": "South-west ruin", "polygon_in": [[4,18],[14,18],[14,24],[4,24]], "features": ["..."]},
    {"name": "West craters", "circle_in": {"x": 3.5, "y": 30, "diameter": 5}, "features": [
      {"name": "Crater", "category": "exposed", "circle_in": {"x": 3.5, "y": 30, "diameter": 3}}]}]}
  ```
  An area gives exactly one of `polygon_in`, `circle_in` or `objective`; a feature gives `polygon_in` or `circle_in`.
- **State.** `GameState::terrain`, the areas in file order (JSON `"terrain"`, omitted when empty, so every older file
  loads and saves byte for byte).
- **Validation** (`check_terrain_data`, shared by the loader and the state JSON; `DataError`):
  - a polygon that is not convex, has fewer than 3 or more than 16 vertices, or has no area;
  - a circle with a diameter of 0 or less;
  - an area not wholly on the battlefield, or a feature not wholly within its area;
  - an area with no features or more than 16;
  - a Dense feature without a height above 0, or a height on another category;
  - an empty name;
  - more than 32 areas;
  - an objective link without a mission, to an unknown objective, or to one already linked;
  - any unit of the scenario set up on a Light or Dense feature.
- **Custom battles** take the terrain block from the terrain scenario as they take its mission block (D-084): the
  client's army builder and the arena's `--terrain <scenario>`. Without one they are as before.
