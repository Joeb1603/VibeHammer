## D-086: The bot plays the mission: area sets in the army plan, the objective terms in the move score, the approach to an area (M87; amends D-083 part 5 and D-084's Bot note)
**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (M87's plan mode; design approved by the user, with two readings agreed there: the mission
stays symmetric, a random-army gap is recorded as open; "200 games a seat" is 200 games with each side going first).
Bot and arena only: no rule, engine, state, action, event or file format change. Core gains one read-only geometry
query (`within_objective` / `objective_gap_mm`, which `model_within_objective` now calls: the same result).

**Why.** M86 gave the battles the D-084 mission, but the bot ignored the objectives: it played the objective scenario
exactly as the rosters, and the holding side won (the Tyranids 185 of 200, sitting on their home).

**The gate.** Everything below is on only under a mission and with an objective weight above 0
(`objectives_on`: `MoveWeights::objective`, `objective_approach` or `objective_fire`). Without a mission (V1, both bot
scenarios, Strike, the rosters, the m46/m68/m73/m77 replays) the bot plays as at M86, and with every objective weight
0 a mission game plays as at M86 too (the arena proves both, `docs/dev/bot_arena.md`). The plan's area sets come after
its groups and leave them as they were, so shooting and charges, which read only the groups, are unchanged by them.

- **The VP rate** (`vp_share`, `normal/value.*`). One VP in shares: the points one kill VP stands for (`kill_points /
  kill_vp`; `kill_points` when kill VP is 0) over the mean points of every unit (`share_weight`'s mean). Then the move
  score's share margin plus objective VP at this rate is the VP margin in shares (kills give 1 VP per 50 points). On the
  objective scenario 50 / 136.8 = 0.37 shares a VP: the Centre is worth 1.8 shares a scoring, a home 0.73.
- **Who scores next** (`next_scorings`, `normal/objectives.*`). Seen from our turn: the enemy's next Command phase reads
  control at the end of our turn (a move can deny it); ours reads it at the end of the enemy's next turn (a move must
  hold through it). Each counts from the mission's first scoring round, and ours only with a turn of ours to come.
- **The enemy's level of control at the end of its next turn** (`objective_outlook`, and the plan by each later turn):
  a distribution (`LevelPmf`), each enemy unit with all its OC and its chance of having a model in range: 1 within
  range or within its Move; else the likelier of its advance roll covering the gap and a charge after a Normal move
  (onto a unit of ours standing there). **One place** (`one_place`): an enemy unit within range of an area stays there;
  otherwise its chances over the areas are scaled to sum to at most 1. *Measured:* without it every reachable unit
  counted at every area, so an area the enemy could reach looked lost and a home looked threatened by the whole enemy
  army: the Chaos Space Marines sent three units to hold their home and none to the Centre (traced, seed 1).
  OC '-' (a battle-shocked unit, 01.07) adds nothing: it is still battle-shocked at both checks before the next scoring.
- **The objective terms** (`objective_terms`, added in `score_impl` after the team terms, so every other sum is as before).
  For the move's end positions, the unit's OC within range of each area:
  - *deny*: the enemy's VP there at the rate, x (they would control it with our other units' level, and wouldn't with
    ours added): a step, since the enemy's level at the end of our turn is known (our kills this turn are ignored);
  - *score*: our VP there at the rate, x (P(the enemy's level < ours with the unit) - P(... without it)), the unit's OC
    scaled by the share of it left after the enemy's turn on the line (`us_left`, read from `hold_line` /
    `enemy_turn_from` / `engaged_line` without changing any value);
  - our other units count where they stand, except a unit the plan sends to that area this turn and that hasn't moved
    yet: all its OC (the promise, so the members of an area set coordinate);
  - both x `MoveWeights::objective` (default 1).
- **Area sets** (`ArmyPlan::areas`, `UnitPlan::area`, `AreaSet`, `army_plan.*`). Under a mission, in our turn after
  our Command phase's scoring, after the groups: of our units with OC that are in no group and unengaged (the pool), the
  4 that reach the area soonest (the plan's travel), then by OC per strength, form sets of 1 to 3. A set's value: over
  our scorings after its arrival (three at most, within the game), its VP at the rate x the gain in P(our level beats the
  enemy's level by then) over our units outside the pool where they stand; plus, arriving this turn, the enemy scoring
  it denies. A set must be worth more than 0.05 (`kMinPlanValue`); within an area the best by value per risk (the
  members' strength), a bigger set only if it adds 0.05 (`kMinPlanGain`); across areas greedily by value per risk, one
  set per area and one area per unit. So the cheapest unit that holds an area holds it, and the best area goes first.
  No opportunity cost: the pool's units have no paying group, and they still shoot from an area. `describe` adds each
  set ("Havocs 1 to hold A home (+2.79, 100%)": hold if we control it now, contest if its value is mostly the scoring it
  denies, else take). A last unit gets an area too (the groups' early exit with one unit stays as it was).
- **Heading for the area** (`make_area_approach`, `approach_term`). A unit with no followed group, unengaged, with an
  area set: `objective_approach` (default 0.1) x the area's value a scoring x the turns saved towards it beyond its
  Move (centroid to the area's edge, M + 3.5" a turn). It replaces D-083 part 5's lone approach for such a unit; a unit
  with no area keeps the lone approach (unchanged without a mission). Its line stays the closest enemy unit's (no
  partners, no support). Its candidates line up on the area's centre, and the labels and reasons name the area: "Advance
  on Centre (take)", "Normal move 5\" towards A home (hold)".
- **Spots on the areas** (`move_candidates(..., area_spots)`): for the unit's area and every area its move can reach, a
  translation straight at the centre (as far as it goes) and a line abreast there facing the closest enemy unit (two
  ranks too when the centre is within the move), appended after the other candidates (whose order is unchanged).
- **The last unit's survival** under the mission is weighed on the VP result (D-084: VP first, points destroyed on equal
  VP), not on points destroyed alone.
- **The escape guard** (`escape_stands`) ranks with the default weights but the caller's objective weights, so with
  them 0 it is M86's.
- **Arena.** `--weights` keys `objective`, `objective_approach`, `objective_fire`; `--mission <scenario>` (custom and
  random battles read their mission block from it; for tuning); the mission summary adds each area's mean VP per side;
  a new line, the largest melee (the most units in one connected group of engagements at a Fight phase's start, and as
  a share of the units on the battlefield, each game's maximum, averaged). The `--per-game` line is unchanged.

**Tuning (M87).** Release arena, the bot on both sides; "Chaos" is the Chaos Space Marines' (Side A's) share of the
games, each variant with each side going first.
- *The enemy unit in one place* (`one_place`) went in first: without it the plan sent three Chaos Space Marine units to
  hold their own home and none to the Centre (seed 1 traced).
- *On M86's numbers* (Centre 5 VP), 100 seeds a side first (200 for the defaults): the defaults gave the Chaos Space
  Marines 1.2% (the Tyranids took the Centre, 13–17 VP a game, nearly uncontested); `objective` 0.5 gave 2%, 2 gave
  3.5%; `objective_fire` 1 gave 2%. Random armies, defaults: 22.2%.
- *The mission's numbers* decided it, not the weights (D-084's Tuned note has the table: three middle areas 8.5%,
  Centre 3 / 2 / 1 / 0 VP 5% / 9.5% / 25% / 57.5%, homes 4 / 8 with 1 VP per 25 points and Centre 1 / 2 32.5% / 23%;
  random armies 20–22% whatever the mission). The user chose the small Centre (Centre 1, homes 4 / 8, 1 VP per 25
  points).

**Open** (PLAN.md, M87): the balance (the objective scenario 31–34% for the Chaos Space Marines against 40%; random

armies, or terrain (Later, item 1).
