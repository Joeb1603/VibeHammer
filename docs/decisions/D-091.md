## D-091: Objective Consolidation and [IGNORES COVER] (12.08, 24.18; M90; amends D-022, D-071)
**Decided:** 2026-11-19 (M90's plan mode; the design approved by the user, and three test questions answered by the
user during the milestone, below). **Implemented:** M90.

### 12.08 Objective Consolidation: the readings
The rule text (`docs/rules/02_battle_round/12_fight_phase.md`, 12.08) with 14.01, 14.02 and D-009:
- **The mode.** The modes keep their order: Ongoing if the unit is engaged, else Engaging if an enemy unit is within 3",
  else Objective if one or more of the mission's objectives are within 3".
  - A unit is within 3" of an objective if one of its models is (D-009). The distance is measured to the closest part
    of the area (14.01), edge to edge from the base: `objective_gap_mm` (the area as a round "base" of its diameter,
    D-084) at most 3" + EPS_MM.
  - A unit already within range of an objective is within 3" of it.
  - Without a mission there are no objectives, so there is no Objective mode and play is as before (D-022).
- **The choice.** The player selects exactly one of those objectives, by its index in `GameState::mission->objectives`
  (as `ObjectiveControlChanged` does). `legal_actions` offers one `StartConsolidation{unit, {}, objective}` per
  objective in range.
- **While Moving.** Each model that is moved must end within range of the objective, or closer to it than at its start:
  `more_than(gap before, gap after)`, measured from its start pose. As D-074 part 2, a turn does not count as moving
  closer.
  - A model that starts within range can't get closer, so it must stay within range.
  - "Within range if possible, or closer if not" is left to the player: `TODO(if-possible)` (CLAUDE.md).
- **After Moving.** The unit is within range of the objective, i.e. one or more of its models (D-009). A move that fails
  this is reverted, as D-022's are, and the unit may try again.
- **Who.** Both players' units consolidate this way (12.07), the non-active player's included.
- Only Engaging Consolidation forces fights; an Objective Consolidation forces none.

### 24.18 [IGNORES COVER]
- "The target cannot have the benefit of cover against that attack (13.08)." It is decided when the targets are
  selected, where D-089 decides the benefit of cover. `make_weapon_selections` never sets
  `WeaponSelection::benefit_of_cover` for a profile with the ability, so the BS is not worsened. Stealth-like sources of
  cover (24.33, not implemented) will be covered by the same check.
- It is used up at target selection, so it is not an attack-time ability (D-006): it does not split identical attacks.
- The Flamer (Legionaries, Havocs) and the Heavy flamer (Chaos Terminators) are [IGNORES COVER] and [TORRENT], so the
  cover's BS never mattered for them. They are no longer refused (D-071). So the Havoc Champion keeps its BSData default
  Flamer, the Terminators' "Heavy weapon" model type can be taken, and both join the builder's options and the random
  armies' draws.
- *Old files:* a file saved before M90 holds the ability as `{"kind": "Unknown", "text": "Ignores Cover"}`. By D-071's
  re-parse rule it loads as the ability now. No file in `tests/` or `data/scenarios` holds one.

### In code (M90)
- **State, actions, events.**
  - `StartConsolidation`, `ConsolidationStarted` and `MoveInProgress` have an optional `objective` (an index). JSON
    omits it when empty, so every older file loads and saves byte for byte.
  - `ConsolidationStarted` also carries the objective's `objective_name` for the log, as `ObjectiveControlChanged`
    does (omitted when empty).
  - A state whose move names an objective the mission doesn't have is refused as `DataError`.
- **Core queries** (`state/objectives.hpp`): `objectives_within`, `objective_area_pose` and `move_objective`.
- **Core rules** (`rules/fight.hpp`): `consolidation_objective_options`, `consolidation_choice_problem` (it replaces
  `consolidation_targets_problem`) and `ends_within_or_closer_to_objective`.
- **`move_reach`.** One required group against the area's round pose: within range (threshold EPS_MM) or closer
  (threshold `before - EPS_MM`), as circles for a round mover and ReachShapes for an oval one.
- **`move_end_problems`.** "12.08: unit N must end its Objective Consolidation within range of Centre".
- **Refusals:**
  - "12.08: Far is not an objective within 3\" of unit 1 (Objective Consolidation)";
  - "... has no enemy unit within 3\" (Objective Consolidation), so it selects an objective, not enemy units";
  - "... is engaged (Ongoing Consolidation), so it selects enemy units, not an objective".
- **The client.**
  - Clicking the unit starts the consolidation when one objective is in range; with several, a menu names them (with
    Cancel).
  - The board outlines the objective in gold during the move.
  - The text: "Consolidate: Chaos Terminator Squad onto Centre" and "[12.08] unit 3 consolidates (Objective, Centre)".
  - The skip (D-047, D-049) and the Auto move (D-050) measure the gap to the area. A model within range counts as in
    base contact, so a unit with every model in the area skips the step.
  - The Auto move tries a third arrangement for an objective: the whole unit moved straight towards the area's centre by
    what its closest model needs. It keeps the formation, and so coherency, where the greedy passes can't (e.g. a row
    2.9" from the area). The best of the three that pass is kept.
  - [IGNORES COVER] has its label and help (24.18), and the Fight phase shows it as having no effect there.
- **The bot.**
  - Normal takes an Objective Consolidation when its Auto move adds models within range of the area. The most models
    added wins; ties go to the first offered. The other units' consolidations are weighed as before and go first. The
    defaults do the same.
  - M91 weighs it with the objective terms.
  - The odds (`unit_ranged_volley`) don't worsen an [IGNORES COVER] profile's BS for `ShotContext::target_cover`.
  - `state_hash` adds the objective behind its own tag only when set.
- **The arena.** A mission run's summary adds "objective consolidations a game (M90): A x, B y", counted from committed
  Objective Consolidations. The `--per-game` line is unchanged.

### Tests and replays changed on purpose
- D-022's test (`R12_08_ConsolidationMove.AUnitThatMeetsNoModeCannotConsolidate`) keeps its case without a mission. A
  twin with an objective under the unit now gets Objective Consolidation.
- The refusal pins (D-079's refused list and defaults, the builder's, D-069's and D-071's counts) no longer list the
  flamers.
- `tests/replays/m87_objectives_bot_game.json` was regenerated. At step 550 Side B's unit in the Centre gets a
  Consolidate decision; the file gains those steps only (6 lines), and its checks still hold at seed 29. Every other
  replay is byte-identical.
- New: `tests/replays/m90_objective_consolidation.json` (scripted, embedded initial state, seed 1). The Chaos
  Terminators destroy two Termagants south of the Centre, consolidate onto it, and Side A controls it from the end of
  that Fight phase.
- *The user's choices* (2026-11-19). The random armies now draw flamers, which moved three tests:
  - `M77_BotDeploy.LongRangeShootersStandBackWhileInRange` now asserts D-081's own rule: a chosen spot out of range is
    no further back than the proposal. It used to assert that an out-of-range proposal never gives way to a spot
    further back, but D-081 puts in-range spots first, so a spot further back and sideways that is in range rightly
    wins.
  - `M77_BotDeploy`'s random battles were widened from 12 to 24 seeds.
  - The melee nudge bar moved from more than a third to more than 30%. On the M90 draws 56 of 176 melee set-ups
    (31.8%) end nearer the enemy; its hard checks are unchanged.
  - `M89_Regression.AtCoverZeroTheTerrainScenarioPlaysAsAtM88` became `...PlaysAsTheM88BotUnderM90Rules`, pinned to
    the M90 binary at cover 0. The terrain scenario has the mission, and Objective Consolidation gives Side B one more
    consolidation in that game, so M88's hashes can't be reproduced under M90's rules.
