## D-098: The rule numbers of Explosives and Crushing Impact: the PDF's, 15.05 and 15.06 (before M97)
**Decided:** 2026-11-26 (the user's ruling, after M96; asked when M97 was proposed). Numbering only: no rule's text
changes.

### The conflict
The two sources of the core rules number these two stratagems the other way round:
- the PDF (`docs/rules/03_battlefields_and_tactics/15_stratagems.md`, page 56): **15.05 Explosives**, **15.06 Crushing
  Impact**;
- the app, as `docs/gdmissions/` scraped it (`core_rules/15_stratagems.md`, and its own changelog,
  `changelog/core_rules_changelog.md`): 15.05 Crushing Impact, 15.06 Explosives.

Their text is the same word for word; only the two numbers differ. Of the 156 numbered rules, these are the only two
whose numbers disagree (every other difference between the two indexes is in a title's wording, e.g. "Battle-shock"
against "Battle-shock step").

### The evidence
- **The PDF's numbers are printed, not extracted.** `tools/extract_rules.py` makes a heading only from a line of the
  PDF's own text that reads "TITLE NN.NN" (its `HEADING` pattern), so it can't number a rule itself. Page 56 extracts
  cleanly, and each card's text sits under its own title.
- **The rules' own cross-reference agrees with the PDF, in both sources.** 12's example (Fights First units and pile-in
  moves) says the MONSTER "destroyed its charge target in the Charge phase using the Crushing Impact stratagem
  (15.06)": `docs/rules/02_battle_round/12_fight_phase.md` and `docs/gdmissions/core_rules/12_fight_phase.md` alike.
  So only the app's headings swap the two.
- The PDF's order follows the phases: Insane Bravery (Command), Explosives (Shooting), Crushing Impact (Charge).

### The ruling
Code, tests and docs cite **15.05 for Explosives** and **15.06 for Crushing Impact** (`R15_05_*`, `R15_06_*`), as the PDF
prints and as the rules' cross-reference has it. A reader of `docs/gdmissions/` finds them the other way round there.

This settles these two numbers only. Any other conflict between the two sources found later is asked about, as for
any rule where `docs/rules/` disagrees with what was expected (CLAUDE.md).
