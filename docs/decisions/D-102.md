## D-102: [LETHAL HITS], [PSYCHIC], [CLEAVE X], [ONE SHOT] and the keyword-conditioned abilities (24.23, 24.29, 24.06, 24.26, 24.01; M100; amends D-006, D-071, D-101)
**Decided:** 2026-11-27 (M100), **by Claude without the user** (the Phase 1 batch, `PLAN.md`). Every choice the rules
leave open is marked **(own decision)** and is open to the user's review.

### 1. What M100 takes
The weapon abilities left after M99 that need no new shooting type and no CHARACTER group:
- **[LETHAL HITS]** (24.23);
- **[PSYCHIC]** (24.29);
- **[CLEAVE X]** (24.06);
- **[ONE SHOT]** (24.26, with the app's 24.26.01);
- **the keyword-conditioned forms** of 24.01 ("[LETHAL HITS: VEHICLE]", "[SUSTAINED HITS 1: INFANTRY/BEASTS]").

In the BSData catalogues of the pinned commit: Psychic 59 profiles, Lethal Hits 44, the conditioned forms 10, One Shot
7, Cleave 4. On the datasheets in `data/units/` it frees the Balefire tome, the last roster weapon still refused.

Left out (they stay refused, D-071): [PRECISION] and [INDIRECT FIRE] (the next milestone), a random X ("[SUSTAINED HITS
D3]", "[RAPID FIRE D3]": 17 profiles) and a random A on a melee weapon (3 of 655 melee profiles; its attacks can't be
split before they are rolled, and the client's and the bot's melee plans count a known A).

### 2. [LETHAL HITS] (24.23)
"Each time an attack made with a [LETHAL HITS] weapon results in a critical hit, you can choose for that attack to
automatically wound the target." The designer's note: no wound roll is made for it, so it can't be a critical wound and
can't trigger [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]. App 01.05.01: an automatic wound "is not a critical wound".
- **Each critical hit wounds automatically.** No wound roll is made for it. The wound rolls are made for the other hits
  (the additional hits of [SUSTAINED HITS] included: they are hits of their own).
- **The choice (own decision).** Taking the automatic wound is at least as good as rolling unless a critical wound does
  something more, which only [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] does. So:
  - without [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] on the profile, the automatic wound is taken without a question;
  - a profile with both [LETHAL HITS] and [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] is **refused when selected**, with a rule-cited reason
    (D-026's principle: never resolve it wrongly). No weapon profile in the pinned catalogues has both. The question
    becomes real when abilities grant [LETHAL HITS] (the roadmap's Phase 4), and the decision is built then.
- **State.** `WoundRolls::automatic` (the automatic wounds; `wounds()` counts them, `critical_wounds()` does not) and
  `AttackFlow::automatic_wounds` (set when the batch's hit rolls stand, until its wound rolls do). Both are omitted from
  JSON at 0.
- 15.09 snap shooting's hits are not critical hits (D-097), so they are never lethal; [TORRENT]'s automatic hits neither.

### 3. [PSYCHIC] (24.29)
"Each time an attack is made with a [PSYCHIC] weapon, you can ignore any or all modifiers to that attack's BS or WS
characteristic and any or all modifiers to the hit roll." App 02.02.02: "you can still choose to apply
positive/beneficial modifiers while ignoring negative/detrimental modifiers".
- **(own decision)** Every modifier that makes the attack worse is ignored and every one that makes it better is kept;
  that is always the attacker's best choice, so no question is asked:
  - ignored: 13.08's benefit of cover (it worsens the BS, D-089), 10.06's and 17.03's -1 to the hit roll (D-073);
  - kept: [HEAVY]'s +1 (24.16).
- 15.09 snap shooting is not a modifier ("only hits on an unmodified hit roll of 6 irrespective of ... any modifiers"),
  so a psychic snap shot still needs a 6.
- "Psychic attacks" (for rules that name them) are the attacks of a profile with the ability; it stays one of the
  profile's attack-time abilities, so D-006 keeps psychic attacks apart.

### 4. [CLEAVE X] (24.06)
"Each time you gather attack dice for a [CLEAVE] weapon, if you only selected one target for all of that weapon's
attacks, add X additional attack dice for every five models that were in the target unit in the Select Targets step
(rounding down)."
- As [BLAST] (24.05), from the models recorded at Select Targets, but only when the weapon's attacks are not split
  (04.02 Splitting Melee Attacks).
- A new target (04.03.03) is a new Select Targets step, so the models are counted again.

### 5. [ONE SHOT] (24.26, 24.26.01)
"Each weapon with this ability can only be selected to make attacks with once per battle."
- **State.** `Weapon::one_shot_spent` on the model's own weapon (each model has its own copy; two of the same weapon on
  a model are two weapons, 24.26.01). Omitted from JSON when false.
- **When.** It is set when the unit's attacks start with the weapon selected (Shoot, Fight, a snap shot), whether or
  not an attack is then made.
- **After.** A [ONE SHOT] profile of a spent weapon is refused with "24.26: ..." and is no shot or melee option; a
  model whose only melee weapons are spent can't make melee attacks (D-021).
- **(own decision)** The ability is the weapon's when one of its profiles has it; a profile of the same weapon without
  it could still be selected.
- "If a destroyed model is returned to a unit" and "a new unit is added" need rules the engine doesn't have yet.
- The bot fires a [ONE SHOT] weapon as soon as it is worth firing; it does not save it for a better target.

### 6. The keyword-conditioned forms (24.01)
"If a weapon ability is followed by one or more keywords, when making attacks with that weapon, that ability only
applies if the target unit has one or more of those keywords."
- **Parsed for the abilities that act from the hit roll on:** [LETHAL HITS], [SUSTAINED HITS X] and [DEVASTATING
  WOUNDS] (the ones the catalogues have). The ability holds its condition (`TargetCondition`: the keywords, and "non-"
  as D-101 reads [ANTI]).
- **Applied when the batches are gathered:** an ability whose condition the target doesn't meet is left out of the
  attack's profile; one it meets is kept without the condition, so identical attacks stay identical (D-006).
- **(own decision)** A condition on any other ability (one that acts when weapons, targets or dice are selected) is not
  known: its text stays unknown and the profile is refused (D-071).
