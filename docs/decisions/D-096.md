## D-096: Reaction windows and the first stratagems: a UseStratagems decision just after or just before a roll, spending CP (15.01), Command Re-roll (15.02), Insane Bravery (15.04); damage rolled per attack; format-3 replays (M95; amends D-011, D-014, D-023, D-025, D-033, D-039, D-069, D-072, D-095)
**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (M95, the user: one milestone; all replays regenerated as format 3; stratagems offered to a
human by default with a setting to stop the offers; the bot uses them behind arena keys, on by default only if they
measure better). Roadmap Phase 0 item 3. Written before the code (design first); the results are added at the end.

### 1. The window (01.03.02 Rules Sequencing, app-only)
A **window** is a timing at which a player can use one or more of their rules. In M95 the only such rules are
stratagems, and the timings are "just after you make a roll" (15.02) and "just before you make a battle-shock roll"
(15.04).
- The engine offers a window as `decision::UseStratagems{timing, unit}`. The answers are `action::DeclineStratagems`
  (listed first) or `action::UseStratagem{stratagem, unit, die}`, which is never a template.
- A window opens only when its side has a stratagem it can use there: enough CP, the 15.01 restrictions, the TARGET,
  01.07, and a die that can still be re-rolled. Otherwise nothing is asked, and play is exactly M94's.
  - A "roll" made with no dice has nothing to re-roll, so no window: [TORRENT]'s automatic hits, wound rolls with no
    hits, a reused advance roll (D-005), or a batch with no attack dice.
  - A window opens even when every die passed. The rules allow it, and the engine doesn't judge usefulness; the
    bot and the player do.
- **Rules Sequencing** orders a window's sides: the active player first, then the opposing player. Mandatory rules,
  such as hazard rolls, are the engine's own flow and come before both. In M95 only one side can have a usable
  stratagem at any timing (the roller, or the active player for Insane Bravery), so the order is tested on its own
  function. "Rules newly usable mid-sequence wait until the rest of that timing resolves" has no case yet.
- `FlowState::window` holds the open window: its timing, roll, unit, batch, the sides still to answer, the roll's
  dice and the dice already re-rolled. It is set only while its decision waits, never at any other decision.
  `compute_decision` and `advance` check it first.

### 2. Using a stratagem (15.01, app-only 15.01.01-03)
- A stratagem costs its CP. CP never goes below 0, and with too little CP it can't be used. Starting CP stays 0
  (D-023).
- Each player can't use the same stratagem twice in one phase, and can't target the same unit with two stratagems
  in one phase. "That unit or model" counts as the unit.
- `GameState::stratagems` keeps, per side, the stratagems used and units targeted this phase, plus once-per-battle
  uses.
  - The per-phase records clear on leaving each End of Phase step.
  - The once-per-battle records never clear.
  - It is omitted from JSON when empty and hashed by the bot only when set.
- 01.07: a player can't target their own battle-shocked unit with a stratagem. This bars Command Re-roll on that
  unit's rolls, including the hazard rolls of its forced Desperate Escape, and bars Insane Bravery on it (app FAQ).
- The sequence is: select the target, reduce the CP, resolve the effect. The events come in that order:
  `StratagemUsed{side, stratagem, unit, cost, cp_left}`, then the effect's.

### 3. Command Re-roll (15.02, 1CP)
- **When.** Just after its player makes one of these rolls for one of their units:
  - an Advance roll (09.06) or a Charge roll (11.02);
  - a Hazard roll, from a Desperate Escape (09.07) or [HAZARDOUS] (24.15);
  - a random-A roll (04.03, D-069);
  - Hit, Wound or Save rolls;
  - a Damage roll (section 5).

  Saves are the defender's rolls for its own unit, so that window belongs to the opposing player. A battle-shock roll
  is never re-rolled.
- **Which die.**
  - One die is picked, any die, a success included.
  - A charge roll is re-rolled in full, with no die picked.
  - A roll made by adding several dice together would be re-rolled whole (01.05.02). No random A or D in the data
    has more than one die.
  - A die already re-rolled can't be picked (01.05.02, D-011): [TWIN-LINKED]'s choice comes first (D-072), then the
    window. With Twin-linked available, Command Re-roll can only reach a die that Twin-linked could have re-rolled
    for free, so this order removes no useful choice.
- **Effect.** The die is rolled again and replaces the first. Then modifiers apply (D-011): criticals and automatic
  failures read the new unmodified die, and sustained hits and hazard mortal wounds are counted again.
- **Engine: one mechanism for every roll.**
  - The faces are rolled and the roll's M94 event is emitted unchanged.
  - Then `after_roll` either opens the window, with the faces parked in it, or applies the roll at once.
  - Applying is M94's consequence code, with the typed result rebuilt from the faces by the same judge function the
    roll uses. Judging draws no dice, so the dice order is unchanged.
  - The re-roll emits `DiceRerolled{roll, unit, die, before, after, result}`, where `result` is the roll's own event
    rebuilt from the new faces. The client shows it without judging anything (D-024).
  - Closing the window applies the roll.
  - Twin-linked's M94 park is unchanged.

### 4. Insane Bravery (15.04, 1CP, once per battle)
- The window comes just before each battle-shock roll of the 08.03 step, for a unit that isn't battle-shocked, so
  one at or below half-strength.
- Used: the roll is automatically successful (01.05.01), so no dice are rolled. The test is done, with the event
  `BattleShockPassedAutomatically{unit}`, and the next unit's window or roll follows.
- Never for the 09.07 roll after a Desperate Escape: its WHEN is the Battle-shock step.

### 5. Damage rolled per attack (amends D-069, replaces D-095 part 3)
- Each attack that inflicts damage with a random D has its own `RollDice{Damage}` (the attacker's), made once its
  model is selected (D-069), then its window, before the model loses the wounds.
- The engine keeps the batch's damage rolls (`BatchInProgress::damage_rolls`) and the casualty order until the batch
  resolves. `plan_damage` replays the unchanged `inflict_damage` on a copy of the target with the stored rolls to
  find the next damaging attack (and its model).
- When none is left, the batch resolves from the stored rolls. It emits `SavesResolved`, `ModelDestroyed` and
  `UnitDestroyed` at the end, exactly as M94, so the target changes at the end as before.
- Each roll also emits `DamageRolled{attacker, target, model, damage, roll}`, the only new event in a game without
  stratagems.
- The k-th roll is drawn where M94's single pass drew it, so the dice order is unchanged. The pre-roll card shows
  one attack's dice, which closes M94's open item.

### 6. The closure, extended (amends D-095 part 2)
- `apply_action` and `start_game` (every overload) answer every RollDice and decline every window inline, so the
  bot's simulator, every existing test and format-1 play are M94's: the same dice in the same order, the same state,
  the same events but `DamageRolled`. Declining emits nothing.
- `apply_action_paced` stops at windows.
- Proof: the M94 and M95 event dumps of every replay (`D095_Closure.DISABLED_DumpReplayEvents`, with
  `WH40K_EVENT_DUMP_WITHOUT=DamageRolled`) are identical. `D096_Closure` checks paced play with every window
  declined against inline play for every replay, for random games, and for each roll point with scripted dice.
- Read-only queries: `window_pending(state)`, and `window_info(state)`, which gives the side, the CP, the usable
  stratagems and their costs, and the dice with the ones that can be picked.

### 7. Format 3 (amends D-014, D-025, D-095 part 5)
- Format 3 records each window's answer as a step, with one Damage step per damaging attack. `Match::to_replay` and
  saves write 3.
- `with_roll_steps` keeps its name and returns format 3:
  - **format 1:** after the start and after each step, every roll is made and every window declined, each
    recorded;
  - **format 2:** step by step. Each recorded step is replayed paced, with any window before it declined. After a
    recorded Damage roll, the batch's further Damage rolls are made, since M94 made them in one. Windows waiting at
    the end are declined, but a pending roll is not made (a save can stop at one).
- `run_replay`, the replay viewer, saves and `M88_Replay` convert formats 1 and 2 this way.
- All 29 replays were regenerated as format 3: the same description, initial state and `expect`, and the same steps
  once the roll and decline steps are taken out.
- `tests/data/legacy/` gains format-2 copies of m8 and m62 and two M94 saves (one waiting for a save roll).

### 8. The client and the bot (amends D-033, D-039)
- **GameSession:**
  - a per-side `auto_decline` beside `auto_roll`, defaulting on;
  - the client turns it off for a human side while "Offer stratagems" is on (the default), and for a bot side
    whose config plays stratagems;
  - each automatic decline is a step of its own;
  - Undo never takes back a roll or a decline on its own, but it does take back a `UseStratagem` on its own.
- **Cards** (D-033 amended):
  - With the dice pop-ups on, the rolled card of a roll whose window waits for a human gets "Command Re-roll (1 CP)"
    and clickable dice; its Continue declines, so it costs no extra click.
  - With the pop-ups off, a pop-up asks.
  - The pre-roll card of a battle-shock roll with a window offers Insane Bravery; its Roll declines, then rolls.
  - Critical dice get a gold border.
- **Bot:**
  - Two `MoveWeights` keys, `command_reroll` and `insane_bravery` (0 or 1), with heuristics in
    `normal/stratagems.cpp`.
  - The defaults come from D-092's keep rule (section 9).
  - A side whose keys are off declines through the session, so with them off the bot never sees a window, and the
    arena's identity set keeps its `state` and `trace` hashes.

### 9. Results
- *The closure:* the M94 and M95 dumps of all 29 older replays (every event and the final state,
  `WH40K_EVENT_DUMP_WITHOUT=DamageRolled`) are byte-identical. The `D095_Closure` tests now decline every window: paced
  play equals inline play for every replay without a stratagem, for random games and for each roll point. A game
  that uses a stratagem has no inline twin, so it is checked paced only (`m95_stratagems.json`).
- *Replays:* all 29 regenerated as format 3. A script checked each against `git show HEAD:`: the same initial state and
  `expect`, and the same steps once the roll and decline steps are taken out. They gained 958 decline steps and 8 damage
  rolls; only `m94_paced_rolls.json`'s description changed (its damage is "one per attack since M95").
  - New `m95_stratagems.json` (seed 266, picked by `D096_Replay.DISABLED_SearchM95Seed`). Side B starts with 1 CP.
  - Side A re-rolls a missed hit die (1 to 5) and, as the defender, a failed save (1 to 3).
  - Side B passes a battle-shock test with Insane Bravery, re-rolls a damage roll (D3 1 to 2, killing the Intercessor)
    and a charge roll that reached no target (7 to 8, which reaches it).
  - The legacy saves: the M94 format-2 save loads as the same game played now; the one stopped at a save roll still
    waits for it.
- *Refusals:* 15.01's (CP, once per phase, the unit already targeted), 15.02's (the WHEN, a die out of range, a die picked
  for a charge roll), D-011's ("a die is re-rolled only once") and 01.07's (a battle-shocked unit) are rule-cited.
- *As built, beside the design:*
  - `WindowInfo` also gives the roll a BeforeRoll window comes before (`next_roll`, for Insane Bravery's face-down card)
    and an AfterRoll window's roll judged from its dice now (`result`, for the window's pop-up), so the client judges
    nothing (D-024).
  - The rolled card's decline button reads "Keep the roll". The picked die and the re-rolled die are marked with a blue
    ring and "R", since gold is the critical dice's border.
  - `--pick-dice` picks a window's die too (no separate `--pick-reroll`). `--dice-popups 0|1` sets "Show dice pop-ups"
    for a scripted run.
  - The [TWIN-LINKED] re-roll card shows only the re-rolled dice, so it carries no window: the window after it opens in
    its pop-up, with the batch's dice.
- *Client:* the session declines for its auto sides (`auto_decline`, set from "Offer stratagems" and the bot's keys).
  `StratagemPicks` holds the human's pick. Each dice card carries its roll's identity, and the window's buttons go on
  the rolled card. xvfb screenshots:
  - the hit card with its window buttons, a picked die and two gold criticals;
  - the defender's save card;
  - the window's pop-up, with the dice pop-ups on and off;
  - Insane Bravery on the battle-shock roll's face-down card.
- *Bots:*
  - `command_reroll` and `insane_bravery` (0 or 1), with heuristics in `normal/stratagems.cpp` in model equivalents
    (odds.hpp's unit of value):
    - Command Re-roll takes the die whose re-roll gains the most: a missed hit (worth a fresh attack die), a failed
      wound (worth a hit), a failed save of its own unit, a low damage or random-A roll, or a failed hazard roll. It
      re-rolls it when the gain is at least a quarter of a model.
    - It re-rolls a charge that reaches no target when a new 2D6 reaches one at least a quarter of the time. Advance
      rolls are kept.
    - Insane Bravery is used for a test that fails at least a quarter of the time.
  - With both off, the session declines every window for the bot. The identity set (13 runs, 2,600 games) is then
    identical to the M94 binary in every per-game field but `steps` and `actions`.
  - D-092's head-to-head on the terrain scenario: the variant on each side, each side first, seeds 1-100, 400 games
    against the bot playing no stratagem.

    | Keys on | Wins | Losses | Margin |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `command_reroll` | 222 | 178 | +44 |
    | `insane_bravery` | 204 | 196 | +8 |
    | both | 223 | 177 | +46 |

    Every run was clean (no rejection, revert, fallback or unfinished game). By the keep rule (at least 10 more wins
    of 400, about one standard deviation), **Command Re-roll is on by default and Insane Bravery off**: Insane
    Bravery's +8 equals Gone to Ground's at M93, and with it both add nothing over Command Re-roll alone.
  - With Command Re-roll on, the default bot plays it, so its games change on purpose. The M95 binary at the defaults
    is the arena's reference from here on. With `--weights command_reroll=0` it plays as the identity runs above.
  - The bot-game replays (m46, m68, m73, m77, m87, m88, m89, m91) and the per-game pins of older bots (M78, M87, M89,
    M91) are played by the bot without stratagems (`tests/ai/bot_configs.hpp`), as M91 pinned its keys at 0. Their
    files and pins stayed as they were.
  - In a traced game with both keys on, the bot used 13 stratagems in 5 rounds, within the CP: 2 a round a side.
  - At `--weights command_reroll=0` the final binary repeats the identity runs line for line (spot-checked on V1 and the
    terrain scenario, 400 games).
  - Decision p95 at the defaults, M94 against M95 (100 games, each pair run side by side):

    | Scenario | p95, M94 | p95, M95 | Wall time |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Terrain, Side A first | 18.0 ms | 15.7 ms | +4% |
    | Objectives | 12.3 ms | 10.8 ms | +0% |
    | Random armies on terrain | 14.0 ms | 11.5 ms | +2% |

    The p95 is lower partly because each window answer is a near-instant decision of its own. Far inside the 500 ms
    budget.
  - On the objective scenario (seeds 1-100), with both bots playing Command Re-roll, the Chaos Space Marines won 36
    games instead of 26.
