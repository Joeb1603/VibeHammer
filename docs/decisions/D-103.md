## D-103: [INDIRECT FIRE] with Indirect Shooting, and [PRECISION] (24.19, 10.07, 24.28; M101; amends D-006, D-071, D-088, D-089, D-096, D-101)
**Decided:** 2026-11-27 (M101), **by Claude without the user** (the Phase 1 batch, `PLAN.md`). Every choice the rules
leave open is marked **(own decision)** and is open to the user's review.

### 1. What M101 takes
The last two weapon abilities of the roadmap's Phase 1 list:
- **[INDIRECT FIRE]** (24.19) with **Indirect Shooting** (10.07), a fifth shooting type;
- **[PRECISION]** (24.28), with the app's 24.28.01 ([DEVASTATING WOUNDS]) and 24.37.01 (not on [TORRENT] weapons).

In the BSData catalogues of the pinned commit: Precision 78 profiles, Indirect Fire 25. None is on the eleven datasheets
in `data/units/`, so shipped play does not change.

Left out: a random X ("[SUSTAINED HITS D3]"), a random A on a melee weapon, and the choice of 24.23 with [DEVASTATING
WOUNDS] (D-102). Lone Operative's limit on [INDIRECT FIRE] (24.24) comes with that ability.

### 2. Indirect Shooting (10.07)
"ELIGIBLE IF: Unengaged and did not make an advance move this turn; has one or more [INDIRECT FIRE] weapons."
- **A shooting type** (`ShootingType::Indirect`), offered in 10.02 beside Normal shooting when the unit has such a
  weapon. A unit eligible for both selects one; with Indirect its other weapons shoot as they would in Normal shooting
  ("its other weapons can still target other visible targets").
- **Targets.** "[INDIRECT FIRE] weapons in your unit can target units that are not visible to the attacking model": for
  those weapons the visibility test is skipped (06.01, 13.07, 13.09-13.11; so a Hidden unit can be targeted too). Range
  and 04.02's engagement rule still apply.
- **Each attack of an [INDIRECT FIRE] weapon** (in this shooting type, whether or not its target is visible):
  - *"The target has the benefit of cover against that attack (13.08)":* the selection records it, terrain or not. An
    [IGNORES COVER] weapon still denies it (24.18: "including from rules that give a model or unit the benefit of
    cover"), and a [PSYCHIC] one ignores its worsened BS (D-102).
  - *"You cannot re-roll hit rolls":* Command Re-roll (15.02) is refused for that batch's hit rolls, as for a snap
    shot.
  - *"An unmodified hit roll of 1-5 fails, unless your unit remained stationary this turn and the target is visible to
    one or more friendly units, in which case an unmodified hit roll of 1-3 fails instead":*
    `AttackProfile::unmodified_hit_fails_to` (1 by 05.01; 5 or 3 here), decided at Select Targets per selection. A 6
    is still a critical hit. With "1-3 fails", a 4 or 5 hits only if the modified roll reaches the BS.
- **"Remained stationary this turn"** is the Remain Stationary move type (09.04), recorded in a new turn flag
  (`UnitTurnFlags::remained_stationary`, omitted from JSON when false). **(own decision)** A unit that made a Normal
  move of no distance did not remain stationary.
- **"Visible to one or more friendly units"** is any unit of the attacking unit's side, itself included, one of whose
  models can see the target unit (06.01 with terrain, as shooting judges it).
- **[HEAVY]** applies as in any shooting in your Shooting phase (24.16).
- **A new target** (04.03.03) is a new Select Targets step: the facts are recorded again.

### 3. [PRECISION] (24.28)
"While resolving attacks made with one or more [PRECISION] weapons, at the start of the Allocation Order step (05.03), if
the target unit contains one or more CHARACTER models visible to one or more of the attacking models, the active player
can select one allocation group that contains one of those visible CHARACTER models. If they do, until those attacks are
resolved, or until that CHARACTER group is destroyed (whichever happens first), that CHARACTER group is the current
allocation group."
- **A decision of the attacking unit's player** (`SelectPrecisionGroup`), at the start of 05.03 for a batch whose
  profile has the ability, before the defender declares the allocation order. Its answers are one per visible
  CHARACTER model of the target, and "none" (listed last: taking a character is the usual wish).
  - It is asked only when the target has a CHARACTER group that is not already the only choice: a unit with one
    allocation group has nothing to select.
  - **"The attacking models"** are the models whose weapons are in the batch (its selections). Visibility is model to
    model (06.01 with 13.07-13.11).
- **Effect.** The selected group is the current allocation group for that batch (05.04.01) until it is destroyed; then
  the declared order applies. The defender still declares the order of all groups (05.03's rules are unchanged).
- **With [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]** (app 24.28.01): "Mortal wounds inflicted by a [PRECISION] attack with the [DEVASTATING
  WOUNDS] ability are first applied to the CHARACTER group that was selected ... (if that group is not already
  destroyed)": a critical wound's mortal wounds go to that group's model while it is alive, before 06.02's order.
- **(own decision) A CHARACTER unit today is one whose every model is a CHARACTER** (the unit keyword; model-level
  keywords come with attached units, 19). So the decision appears against a CHARACTER unit of several models; against a
  lone CHARACTER model there is nothing to select. The rule is built for attached units and is ready for them.
- **App 24.37.01:** a profile with [TORRENT] and [PRECISION] or [INDIRECT FIRE] is malformed data; it is refused when
  selected (D-071's principle).
- The bot selects the visible CHARACTER model with the fewest wounds remaining (ties: the first).
