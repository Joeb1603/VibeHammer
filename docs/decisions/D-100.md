## D-100: M97's open items: the selection of 15.05 / 15.06 by clicking on the board, and Crushing Impact priced in the bot's charge planning (M98; amends D-099)
**Decided:** 2026-11-26 (M98). The user asked for M97's open items ("Do the still open tasks"). Written before the code;
the results are added at the end.

### 1. What M98 takes and leaves
M97 (D-099 part 11) left four items open. M98 takes two:
- **the selection on the board** (D-099 part 8 offered only buttons);
- **Crushing Impact in the charge planning** (D-099 part 9: "judged only at the window").

It leaves:

  no folder to attach, so they stay the user's (PLAN.md);
- **Explosives' CP against a later Command Re-roll**, and tuning the thresholds;
- **Explosives in the Start and End of Shooting phase steps**, which have no decision in the engine.

No rule changes. The engine's legal answers stay the only source of what can be picked (D-024).

### 2. The selection on the board (amends D-099 part 8)
At a `SelectModelAndEnemy` decision `BoardInteraction` is active, in a new `Mode::PickEffect`.
- **The actor** is the stratagem's unit (`decision::SelectModelAndEnemy::unit`), fixed as for a charge's targets.
- **Clickable units:**
  - the enemy units of the legal `SelectModelAndEnemy` answers;
  - the actor itself (to choose its model).
  Every other unit is grey (D-029).
- **A click on an enemy unit** submits its answer. With a model chosen, that is the model's pair. Without one, it is the
  first legal model for the unit, the pair the bar's button names (`effect_choices`, D-099).
- **A click on a model of the actor** (`click_model`; `picking_model()` covers the mode) chooses it, if it is in some
  legal pair. A second click on it drops it.
  - A model in no legal pair is dimmed (`model_dimmed`), and a click on it does nothing.
  - With a model chosen, only the enemy units legal with it are clickable; the rest turn grey.
  - Esc and a click on empty board drop the model (`cancel()`), as for a model chosen for removal (D-042).
- **The ring:** the chosen model (`effect_model()`) is filled in gold, with a gold ring outside the unit's yellow
  selection rings, so it stands out from them. D-042's red ring and cross mean removal, so they aren't used here.
- **The line:** while picking, a line follows the cursor from the actor, as for a shooting target (`picking_target()`).
- **Refusals:** hovering a grey enemy unit shows the engine's reason (`validate_action` on the pair), as M88 does for
  shooting targets: "Can't be picked: 15.05: unit 11 is not within 8" of model 8". The reason is for the chosen model's
  pair, or else for the actor's model closest to that unit (01.04 edge to edge).
- **The bar** keeps one button per enemy unit, the same labels and hover outline as M97's. With a model chosen, the
  buttons are that model's pairs (`effect_choices(state, model)`). The prompt is M97's headline, and the hint says what
  to click.
- **Flags:** `--pick-models <id>` also chooses the model at the first selection (screenshots).

### 3. A forecast query in core (D-024)
The bot must know whether the engine would offer Crushing Impact at the end of a charge it is still planning. Its
checks are the engine's: 15.01's CP, once per phase and the unit not yet targeted, 01.07, 15.06's WHEN and its TARGET
(MONSTER/VEHICLE, engaged with an enemy unit). So the engine answers, not the bot.

```cpp
// engine.hpp
[[nodiscard]] std::optional<std::string> forecast_stratagem_problem(const GameState& state, Side side,
                                                                    Stratagem stratagem, WindowTiming timing,
                                                                    std::optional<UnitId> window_unit, UnitId target,
                                                                    std::optional<StratagemMode> mode = std::nullopt);
```
- **What it gives:** the refusal `side` would get using `stratagem` on `target` (in `mode`) in a window of `timing` for
  `window_unit`, were that window opened in `state` as it stands; nullopt if it could use it there.
- **How:** it builds the window and runs `reaction_problem`, the check the window's offers come from.
- **Scope:** only the windows that follow no roll (D-097, D-099). A roll's stratagem or a roll window gives the
  stratagem's WHEN.
- **For the planner:** the state after a planned charge move (`ChargePlan::after`) is in its Charge step, its window
  already declined inline (D-099). `forecast_stratagem_problem(after, side, CrushingImpact, AfterChargeMove, unit, unit)`
  says whether the window there offered it.

### 4. Crushing Impact priced in the charge planning (amends D-099 part 9)
- **The value** (`normal/stratagems.hpp`): `crushing_impact_value(after, unit)` is the gain of the unit's best pair, as
  the window's own valuation gives it (`best_crush_pair`, D-099). That is the enemy's share for Binomial(T, 1/3) less
  the unit's own for Binomial(T, 1/6), each capped at 6, less `kCrushMinGain` (0.05, the CP's worth). It counts when:
  - that is positive (at its window the bot uses the stratagem exactly then);
  - and the forecast allows it.
  Otherwise it is 0.
  - Net, not gross: at the window the bot pays the CP only for a gain above its worth, so the option is worth the
    difference.
- **Where it is added**, only with `weights.crushing_impact > 0`:
  - `charge_prospect` (a new last parameter, `crushing_impact`): to each roll's value, the charge move's end planned
    for that roll. So the charge declaration (`decide_charge`) and the move score's charge term (`refine_move_score`,
    with the move's own weights) count it;
  - `decide_charge_targets`: to each target set's value. Its reason names the term ("Crushing Impact +0.04").
  Heroic Intervention's charges never get it (15.06's WHEN is the player's own Charge phase), so its valuation is
  unchanged.
- **Approximation:** the term is added to the fight's exchange value, not applied to it. The fight that follows is
  valued with the units as the charge move left them. This is second order: the gain is a few tenths of a unit at most.
- **At the defaults** (key off) no term is computed and no value changes. The identity set must be the M97 binary's in
  every field.
- **Measured** by D-092's keep rule (400 games each, the terrain scenario, the variant on each side, each side first,
  seeds 1-100): `crushing_impact` and both keys (with `explosives`), against the defaults. The defaults follow.
