## D-094: M92's open items: per-weapon new targets in the client, objective markers (14.01.01), Gone to Ground in the bot's move score (M93; amends D-084, D-086, D-090, D-093)
**Decided:** 2026-11-23 (M93; the user asked for all three of M92's open items to be done).

### 1. Per-weapon new targets in the client (04.03.03, D-093)
- Core gains a read-only query, `retarget_choices(state)` (`engine/attack_flow.hpp`): each weapon selection waiting on
  a target that is gone, with its eligible new targets, the same list `legal_actions` and `validate_action` use.
- The client's `RetargetPicks` (`logic/retarget_picks`, raylib-free) holds one row per weapon ("Model 3: Bolt Rifle -
  Focused Fire"), each starting at its first eligible unit; a row can pick any of its units or "lost". The action bar
  draws a combo per row, the engine's buttons (all at one unit, none) and "Retarget as picked (N)" when the picks are
  not one of those buttons. Every game change still goes through `GameSession::submit`.

### 2. Objective markers (14.01.01, app-only; amends D-084)
- The app text (D-093's ruling): "If the location point of an objective does not coincide with a terrain area, you must
  denote the location of that objective with a flat, circular marker, 40 mm in diameter ... A model is within range of
  an objective marker while it is within 3" horizontally and 5" vertically of that objective marker. When measuring
  distances to and from an objective marker, measure to and from the closest part of it." (Also in the Rules Appendix,
  `docs/rules/05_reference/25_rules_appendix.md`, OBJECTIVES NOT WITHIN A TERRAIN AREA.)
- *State:* `ObjectiveArea::marker` (JSON `"marker": true`, omitted when false). A marker is held as its range: a circle
  40 mm + 2 x 3" across (`kObjectiveMarkerDiameterMm`, `kObjectiveMarkerRangeMm`), so "within range" (any part of the
  base within the circle, D-009) is the same test as for an area, everywhere it is used (control, scoring, the bot, the
  client's Auto move). The 5" vertical part needs no check in 2D (`TODO(z)`, D-087).
- *Measuring to it* (14.01.01 "the closest part of it"): 12.08's "within 3" of one or more objectives" measures to the
  marker itself, which is 3" inside its range circle, so for a marker it is the same as being within range.
  Objective Consolidation's "closer to it" orders positions the same either way.
- *Scenario format:* an objective with `"marker": true` gives no `"diameter_in"` (a refusal otherwise). A terrain area
  cannot link to a marker objective (14.01.01: a marker is an objective not within a terrain area; a data error).
- *The objective scenario* (`incursion_objectives_44x60.json`, no terrain): its three objectives are markers now (the
  Centre is 7.6" across where it was 12"; the homes 7.6" where they were 6"). Its VP, kills and result are unchanged.
  The terrain scenario keeps them as terrain objectives (its areas are the objectives, 14.01), unchanged.
- *Custom battles* are fought over the terrain scenario's terrain, so they now read the mission block from the terrain
  scenario too (its objectives are its terrain areas); without that file, from the objective scenario (markers) on a
  bare battlefield. The arena does the same: with `--terrain` and no `--mission`, the terrain file's block.
- *Client:* the board draws a marker's 40 mm disc inside its range circle.

### 3. Gone to Ground in the bot's move score (13.11.01, D-093; amends D-090)
- The move score's guess of whether an enemy unit sees us after it moves (`MoveScoreContext::moved_visible`) now takes
  12" for a unit of ours that has gone to ground: every sampled model hidden (as before) and a Dense feature crossing
  the line from each of them to the enemy's centroid (`TerrainSight::dense_between`, an estimate).
- Behind the weight key `gone_to_ground` (0 or 1, arena `--weights gone_to_ground=0|1`). Measured on the terrain
  scenario by M91's head-to-head (the variant against the key at 0 and the reverse, each with `--first A` and `--first
  B`, 100 games each): the variant won 208 of 400 (21 + 39 as A, 82 + 66 as B), +8 games, under one standard deviation
  (about 10). By the keep rule (D-092: on by default only when measured better) it defaults to 0, so the bot plays as
  at M92; it stays for tuning and is tested.

### Measured
- `gone_to_ground`: above (208 of 400; off by default).
- The objective scenario with markers, 200 games at the default weights: Chaos Space Marines win 67 (33.5%; 32.5% at
  M87), Tyranids 133. The Centre stays the Tyranids' (B 1.95 objective VP a game, A 0.08); the homes are held by their
  owners as before. So the markers leave the mission's balance where M87 left it.
