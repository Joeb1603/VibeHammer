## D-095: Engine-paced dice rolls: a RollDice decision at every roll point, answered by the roller; apply_action makes them inline, the game session paces them (M94; amends D-014, D-025, D-033, D-039, D-069, D-072)
**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (M94, the user: one milestone; a human's rolls follow "Show dice pop-ups"; every roll recorded
as a step, with format-2 saves and replays and format 1 migrated on load; a random D rolled once per batch). Roadmap
Phase 0 item 2 (Option 2 of M20). The engine stops at each roll point, so later milestones can put a decision between
a roll and its result (15.02 Command Re-roll: "just after you make" the roll; re-roll abilities; Feel No Pain).

### 1. The roll points and who rolls (05: "if there is more than one dice to roll, make all of those rolls simultaneously")
One `decision::RollDice{roll, unit}` per roll point (`RollKind`), answered by `action::RollDice` with the same roll and
unit (its one legal action; anything else is refused with a rule-cited reason). The roller is the controlling player
of `unit` (01.03).

| Roll | Rule | `unit` (its player rolls) | Made when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advance | 09.06 | the moving unit | after `StartMove{Advance}`, unless the phase's roll is reused (D-005) |
| Hazard | 06.03 (09.07) | the unit making a Desperate Escape | after `StartMove{FallBack, DesperateEscape}`, once per phase |
| BattleShock | 01.07 (09.07) | that unit | after its Desperate Escape is committed (not already battle-shocked) |
| BattleShock | 01.07 (08.03) | each unit the step tests, in unit order | in the Battle-shock step |
| Charge | 11.02 | the charging unit | after `DeclareCharge`; no target within it fails the charge at once |
| RandomAttacks | 04.03 (D-069) | the attacking unit | when a batch with a random A is selected; the batch is announced after |
| Hit | 05.01 | the attacking unit | after the batch is gathered; none for [TORRENT] (made at once, no dice) |
| Wound | 05.02 | the attacking unit | after the hit rolls; none with no hits (made at once, no dice) |
| Save | 05.03 | the target unit (the opposing player) | after the allocation order |
| Damage | 05.04 (D-069) | the attacking unit | after the defender's casualty order, for a random D: one roll per batch |
| Hazard | 06.03 (24.15) | the attacking unit | after its last batch, unless it is gone or no enemy model is left (D-071) |

The state-aware rule of a RollDice (`decision_rule(state, d)`) says why it is made: 24.15 or 09.07 for a Hazard roll,
08.03 or 09.07 for a BattleShock roll; the stateless one gives the roll's own rule. [TWIN-LINKED] re-rolls (D-072)
stay inside `RerollWoundRolls`: that answer makes them, with no Roll.

### 2. The roll closure: two ways to apply, one machine
The engine always stops at a RollDice. `apply_action` and `start_game` (every overload, `begin_attacks` too) answer
each one for its roller from the same dice, inside the same rollback: the M93 behaviour, so the bot's `Simulator`, the
M39 guard and every test that scripts dice on an action are unchanged. `apply_action_paced` and `start_game_paced` stop
there instead. The invariant: an action applied inline equals it applied paced with every RollDice then answered, from
the same dice: the same dice in the same order, the same events, the same state. Each roll is made by the module whose
action made it before (`engine/rolls.cpp` dispatches), and the state that waits between rolls is never set at another
decision: `AttackFlow::rolling` (a selected batch until its wound rolls, with its hit rolls), `BatchInProgress::priority`
(the casualty order while a random D waits), `FlowState::battle_shock_tests` (the battle-shock rolls owed), each
omitted from JSON when empty. So the state at every non-roll decision, and the bots' `state_hash` there, are as before.
- *Proof (M94):* the existing suite passed unchanged; all 28 replays' events and final states (dumped by
  `D095_Closure.DISABLED_DumpReplayEvents`) are byte-identical between the M93 engine and the M94 engine, inline and
  paced; `D095_Closure` checks paced against inline for every replay and for random games; the Release arena's
  `state` and `trace` hashes on the identity set equal M93's (`docs/dev/bot_arena.md`).
- *Read-only queries:* `roll_pending(state)`, and `roll_dice_info(state)` (the roller, the dice, the expression and the
  number needed: what the client's pre-roll card shows, D-024). `max_move_distance_mm` is nullopt while an Advance roll
  waits. `leadership_target(unit)` (01.06) is public for the card.

### 3. A random D: one Damage roll per batch (the user's choice)
After the defender's casualty order (D-013, D-051), a random D's damage rolls (D-069: one per attack that inflicts
damage, after its model is selected) are made together by one Damage roll, which resolves 05.04 as before. A fixed D
needs no roll: the casualty order resolves it at once. The pre-roll card's dice count is the casualty preview's (each D
at its maximum): a lower D can leave more attacks inflicting damage. *Open:* the per-attack split (one roll per
damaging attack), with the windows Command Re-roll and Feel No Pain need, belongs to their milestone.

### 4. What is not a window yet (for 15.02)
Each Roll makes its dice and goes on to their consequence: a Charge roll with no target fails at once; a battle-shock
roll is applied at once; the damage rolls resolve 05.04 at once; a random A is added at once. Command Re-roll needs a
decision between those; the stored results it needs are there for the hit rolls (`rolling.hits`), the wound rolls (the
M61 park), the save rolls and the advance and charge rolls.

### 5. Replays and saves: format 2 (amends D-014, D-025)
`Replay::format` 2 records each roll as a RollDice step (`run_replay` and the replay viewer apply it paced); format 1
(every roll inside its step) still runs inline. `with_roll_steps` converts format 1 to format 2 by playing it through a
paced `Match` (`RollMode::Paced`), recording each roll after the start and after each step. Saves are format 2; a
format-1 save loads with its rolls added as steps, its state check unchanged. All 28 replays were regenerated
(`docs/dev/replays.md`): format 2, the same description, initial state and expect block, and the same steps once the
1009 roll steps are taken out. `tests/data/legacy/` keeps two format-1 replays (m8, m62) and an M93 save as the
migration's fixtures.

### 6. The client and the bot (amends D-033, D-039)
- *GameSession* is paced. A side with `auto_roll` on has its rolls made at once after the action that led to them,
  each its own history step and log entry ("Step 12: Side A RollDice (Hit)"). The client turns it on for a bot's side,
  and for a human's while "Show dice pop-ups" is off; toggling the setting makes a waiting roll at once. Rebuilding a
  format-2 history (undo, load) makes no roll: the steps hold them.
- *The pre-roll card (D-033 amended):* with the pop-ups on, a human's roll waits for the Roll button on its card,
  face down (`roll_prompt`: the same title, purpose and need as the card of its events), and the board waits. Pressing
  Roll makes the roll; its card then tumbles at once and shows the faces. Bot cards keep the paced, cosmetic Roll
  (D-060). `--steps` presses Roll for a waiting roll.
- *Undo (D-039 amended):* a roll is never taken back on its own. `GameSession::truncate` (one rebuild; `undo` is its
  last step) goes back to before the last step that isn't a roll; the Roll button opens no Undo group, so a roll goes
  with the input that led to it. Re-rolling after an undo would give the same dice anyway (D-025).
- *The bot* never decides a RollDice in a live game (the session rolls for it); its fallback answers one if a load or
  an undo leaves one waiting. The arena's per-game `steps` and `actions` change by construction (each roll a step);
  `state` and `trace` do not.
