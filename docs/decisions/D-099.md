## D-099: Explosives (15.05) and Crushing Impact (15.06): Explosives at the Shoot step, a model-and-enemy step after each stratagem, an after-charge-move window, the enemy's mortal wounds first; format-5 replays (M97; amends D-007, D-014, D-025, D-095, D-096, D-097)
**Decided:** 2026-11-26. This is M97's plan mode, held in M96's session at the user's choice; the user gave the rulings
the same day. Written before the code (design first); the results are added at the end. The numbers are the PDF's
(D-098).

### 1. The user's rulings
- **Explosives is offered at the Shoot step** (15.05 WHEN "Your Shooting phase").
  - It is an answer to the Shoot decision (10.02), beside the Shoot templates, each time the player picks the next unit
    to shoot.
  - It is not a window. A pop-up before every shot would be recorded at every Shoot step, and replays would need a new
    format for it.
- **The EFFECT's selections follow the stratagem.**
  - First the stratagem is used on its unit and its CP are paid (15.01 steps 1–2).
  - Then a new decision asks for the EFFECT's model and enemy unit together (15.05 and 15.06, steps 1–2), as Fire
    Overwatch's snap shot follows its stratagem (D-097).
- **Crushing Impact's cap covers each unit.** "(To a maximum of 6 mortal wounds per unit)" limits the enemy unit's
  mortal wounds and the charging unit's, each to 6. With T at most 12, the cap on the unit's own 1s almost never
  matters.
- **Crushing Impact inflicts the enemy's mortal wounds first.**
  - If that leaves the enemy with no models on the battlefield, the game ends at once (D-007) and the 1s are never
    inflicted. This mirrors D-071, which makes no hazard roll once no enemy model is left.
  - So both sides can never lose their last models at once, and `end_game_if_side_wiped_out` keeps treating that as
    impossible.
- **Carried over from D-097:** a stratagem is offered only when it can do something. Here that means a legal (model,
  enemy unit) pair exists.

### 2. Readings settled by the text
- **Eligible to shoot** (15.05 TARGET) is 10.02's definition (on the battlefield, not already selected to shoot this
  phase) plus 09.07 (a unit that fell back this turn isn't eligible to shoot). The engine's check is
  `unit_shoot_problem`.
  - Explosives doesn't select the unit to shoot (10.02 Select Unit), so the unit can still shoot after it.
  - A unit that has already shot can't use it.
- **EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES** is either keyword, singular or plural. This is the app's 02.05.01 Using Keywords: keywords
  separated by slashes mean any one of them, and singular and plural are alike.
  - Keywords are per unit in the engine (`core/model/keywords.hpp`: V1 units never mix keywords), so every model of
    such a unit is an EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES model.
  - In the data: the Intercessor Squad has Explosives; the Chaos Lord and the Legionaries have Grenades.
- **MONSTER/VEHICLE** works the same way. In the data: the Hive Tyrant (T10, W10), the Screamer-killer (T9, W10) and
  the Tyrannofex (T12, W16). There is no VEHICLE.
- **Crushing Impact's WHEN is "your Charge phase".** A Heroic Intervention charge (15.11) is made in the opponent's
  Charge phase, so it never leads to Crushing Impact.
- **No Command Re-roll after either roll.** 15.02's WHEN lists the rolls it can follow: advance, charge, damage,
  hazard, hit, save, wound, and the number of attacks. Neither stratagem's dice are one of them.
- **15.01's records apply as for every stratagem.**
  - Each stratagem targets its unit, so no other stratagem can target that unit in the same phase. Command Re-roll
    can't re-roll the Explosives unit's later shooting rolls, and a MONSTER whose charge roll was Command Re-rolled
    can't use Crushing Impact.
  - Each is used at most once per phase per player.
- **Neither is an attack.**
  - The mortal wounds need no hit, wound or save roll; cover, [IGNORES COVER] and 04.02's target rules don't apply.
  - Explosives is not a ranged attack, so it doesn't count for 13.09 Hidden.
  - Explosives' enemy unit only has to be unengaged, within 8" of the model (01.04 edge to edge, D-009) and visible to
    it. Visibility is 06.01, plus 13.07 with terrain: the engine's `unit_visible_to_model`.
- **Crushing Impact always has an enemy unit.**
  - After a charge move the unit is engaged with every charge target (11.04 AFTER MOVING).
  - The model must be engaged with the enemy unit selected: within engagement range of one of its models (03.04).
  - If the mortal wounds destroy the charge target, the unit keeps Fights First (11.04, until the end of the turn) and
    can still pile in. 12's example has a MONSTER "unengaged as it destroyed its charge target in the Charge phase using
    the Crushing Impact stratagem (15.06)".
- **06.02 Select Model:** each unit's controlling player selects the models, with one casualty order (D-013). They are
  always asked, even when the choice is forced (D-051). Mortal wounds left when the unit is destroyed are lost.
- **Points destroyed** (D-076) count every wound lost. So a unit's own wounds from Crushing Impact count for the
  opponent, as hazard wounds do.
- No FAQ entry covers either stratagem. The app's text is the PDF's word for word; only the numbers differ (D-098).

### 3. Explosives (15.05, 1CP): offered at the Shoot step
- **The offer:** `legal_for(decision::Shoot)` lists one `UseStratagem{Explosives, unit}` for each unit its player can
  target.
  - They come after every Shoot template, so the earlier answers keep their indices.
  - `answers()` lets `UseStratagem` answer the Shoot decision as well as a window.
- **WHEN:** the Shoot step (10.02) of the player's own turn, at the Shoot decision, with no unit selected and no attack
  or effect in progress.
  - In a window, Explosives is refused with its WHEN.
  - The Start and End of Shooting phase steps have no decision in the engine, so it isn't offered there.
- **TARGET:** a friendly unit on the battlefield that:
  - has the EXPLOSIVES or GRENADES keyword;
  - is unengaged;
  - is eligible to shoot (`unit_shoot_problem`);
  - did not make an advance move this turn (`turn_flags.advanced`).
- **15.01 and 01.07:**
  - the player has the 1CP;
  - they haven't used Explosives this phase;
  - the unit hasn't been targeted by another of their stratagems this phase;
  - the unit isn't battle-shocked.
- **Something to do:** some model of the unit has an unengaged enemy unit within 8" and visible to it
  (`explosives_pairs`).
- Each refusal is rule-cited (15.05, 15.01, 10.02, 09.07, 01.07).
- `apply_details` pays the CP and records the use (`StratagemUsed`), then starts the effect (section 5). There is no
  window to continue.

### 4. Crushing Impact (15.06, 1CP): a window just after a charge move
- **The window:** `WindowTiming::AfterChargeMove` (appended).
  - `CommitUnitMove` opens it with `open_window(state, AfterChargeMove, unit)` once the charge move is committed and the
    charge resolved (`resolve_charge(true)`). The window's unit is the charging unit.
  - It opens only if a side can use a stratagem there (D-096), and never once the game is over.
- **WHEN** (`reaction_when`): an AfterChargeMove window in the Charge step (11.02) of the player's own turn, for a
  friendly unit. A heroic charge comes in the End of Charge phase step, so no window opens after it.
- **TARGET** (`reaction_problem`):
  - the window's unit ("that MONSTER/VEHICLE unit"), with the MONSTER or VEHICLE keyword;
  - 15.01's records and 01.07, as for every window stratagem;
  - something to do: an enemy unit engaged with it, and a model of it engaged with that unit (`crushing_impact_pairs`).
- **Use:** `apply_details` pays the CP, closes the window and starts the effect, as Fire Overwatch and Heroic
  Intervention close theirs. No other stratagem has this timing.
- The closure holds: inline play declines the window, which changes nothing.

### 5. The effect: the model and the enemy unit, the roll, the mortal wounds
- **State:** `FlowState::effect`, a `StratagemEffect` in `core/state/stratagems.hpp`.
  - It holds the stratagem, its unit, the model and enemy unit once selected, whether the roll is made, and the mortal
    wounds still to inflict on the enemy unit and on the unit.
  - JSON omits it when absent. The bots' `state_hash` hashes it with its own tag.
- **Decisions**, from `effect_decision`. `advance` and `compute_decision` check it after Fire Overwatch's snap shooter.
  In order:
  1. **The selection:** `decision::SelectModelAndEnemy{stratagem, unit}` (appended), for the unit's player.
     - Its answer, `action::SelectModelAndEnemy{model, enemy}` (appended), names one legal pair. `legal_actions` lists
       every pair, in model order then unit order. Event: `ModelAndEnemySelected`.
     - Explosives: a model of the unit (every model is EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES), and an unengaged enemy unit within 8" of
       it and visible to it.
     - Crushing Impact: an enemy unit engaged with the unit, and a model of the unit engaged with that enemy unit.
  2. **The roll**, made by the unit's player:
     - `RollDice{Explosives}`: six D6. Each 4+ is 1 mortal wound on the enemy unit.
     - `RollDice{CrushingImpact}`: as many D6 as the model's T. Each 5+ is 1 mortal wound on the enemy unit and each 1
       is 1 on the unit, each total at most 6 (section 1).
     - Both kinds are appended to `RollKind`. Event: `StratagemDiceRolled`, with the dice and the mortal wounds after
       the caps.
     - No window follows (section 2), so the roll is applied at once. `apply_roll` and `rerolled_result` treat the two
       kinds as they treat a battle-shock roll (never reached).
  3. **The enemy's casualties:** `OrderCasualties{enemy}` for the enemy's player (06.02), if the enemy unit suffers any
     mortal wounds.
  4. **The unit's casualties:** `OrderCasualties{unit}` for the unit's player, if the unit suffers any and the enemy's
     army still has models on the battlefield (section 6).

  Then `effect_progress` ends the effect, and play returns to the Shoot or Charge decision, with D-007 checked first.
- **Casualties:** `OrderCasualties` during an effect is 06.02; `decision_rule` already says so outside an attack. Its
  apply and `preview_casualties` look at the effect first and reuse `inflict_mortal_wounds_by_priority`, which emits
  `MortalWoundsInflicted`, `ModelDestroyed` and `UnitDestroyed`.
- `end_game` clears the effect like the other flows.
- **Pure queries** in `core/rules/stratagem_effects.hpp`, read-only, also for the client and the bots:
  - `explosives_pairs` and `crushing_impact_pairs`, with their rule-cited pair problems;
  - `judge_explosives` and `judge_crushing_impact`: the mortal wounds a set of dice inflicts, with the caps;
  - `is_explosives_or_grenades(unit, model)`, which joins `is_monster_or_vehicle` in `keywords.hpp`.

### 6. The wipe-out order (amends D-007)
D-007 ends the game "after the current attack sequence or move resolves" when a side has no models left. A stratagem's
effect is resolved whole before that check, as an attack is, with one exception from section 1: Crushing Impact's 1s
are not inflicted when its 5+s leave the enemy with no models. The game then ends with the charging side the winner,
as when its attacks wipe the enemy out. Both sides losing their last models at once stays impossible.

### 7. Format 5 (amends D-014, D-025, D-095, D-096, D-097)
- **Why:** format 5 (`kReplayFormatOwnTurnStratagems`) records the AfterChargeMove windows' answers and what the two
  stratagems lead to.
  - A format-4 file can't hold them. In 8 of the 31 replays a MONSTER ends a charge move in its own Charge phase with CP
    to spend (m60, m61, m63, m65, m68, m73, m87, m91), so a window would wait before the next recorded step.
  - Explosives needs no format change: the Shoot decision's recorded answers stay legal.
- **Conversion:** before each recorded step and at the end, every window of a timing the file's format didn't record is
  declined:
  - format 4: the AfterChargeMove windows;
  - format 3: those and D-097's (EndOfPhase, AfterAttacks);
  - format 2: every window, as now;
  - format 1 plays inline, so every window is declined there.

  `with_roll_steps` returns format 5. `run_replay`, the replay viewer, saves and `M88_Replay` convert formats 1 to 4.
  `Match::to_replay` and saves write 5.
- **The replays:**
  - All 31 are regenerated as format 5. Each is checked to be its format-4 file converted: the same initial state,
    `expect` and steps once the declines are taken out, with only declines added.
  - The M96 and M97 event dumps of the 31 must be byte-identical, since declining a window emits nothing.
  - Legacy fixtures: before the engine changes, M96-format files with a MONSTER charge are copied to
    `tests/data/legacy/` (a replay such as m60, and a session save).
- **New replay:** `m97_stratagems.json` shows both stratagems. It is a rosters or terrain game (CSM Grenades against
  Tyranid MONSTERs), from a seed search as M96's was.

### 8. The client
- **The Shoot step:** each `UseStratagem{Explosives}` answer is a bar button, "Explosives (1 CP): <unit>". The bar
  already shows every concrete answer that has no board unit. Hovering the button outlines the unit in gold, as M96's
  windows do.
- **Declined stratagems:** `GameSession::legal_offered()` is the legal list without the stratagem answers the deciding
  side declines. A side declines when the "Offer stratagems" setting is off, or when it is a bot that doesn't play the
  stratagem (the session's declined list, D-097).
  - The decision panel and `run_auto_actions` use it, so for such a side the empty-step skip (`lone_skip`) and the
    buttons work as before.
  - The arena uses the same list, which keeps the bots' play identical while the keys are off.
- **The selection:** a bar with one button per enemy unit, each using the first legal model for it (for Crushing Impact
  the button shows the model's T). Hovering outlines the enemy unit and the model. `stratagem_picks` gains
  `effect_choices`.
- **The window:** M96's bar. `headline`, `hint` and `decline_label` gain Crushing Impact; today they fall through to
  Command Re-roll.
- **Cards and the log:**
  - the face-down card names each roll's purpose and need: 6 dice needing 4+, or T dice with their 1s and 5+;
  - a rolled card for `StratagemDiceRolled`;
  - `event_text`, `action_text` and `roll_label` cover the new events, actions and rolls;
  - `rules_help` marks EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES as in the game (15.05), and the MONSTER/VEHICLE entry names 15.06.

### 9. The bot
- **Keys:** `explosives` and `crushing_impact` (0 or 1), off until measured. `BotConfig::plays`, `arena_main` and the
  tests' `without_stratagems()` cover them.
- **Valuation:** a new `analysis::mortal_wound_model_equivalents(unit, pmf)`. It gives the model equivalents a
  distribution of mortal wounds costs a unit, by applying them to a copy with `inflict_mortal_wounds`, and is turned
  into shares through `unit_share`. `hazard_model_equivalents` stays as it is: its sums are part of the identity proofs.
- **Explosives:** at the Shoot decision, before a shot, the bot takes the unit and enemy unit with the highest expected
  share for six D6 at 4+ (Binomial(6, 1/2)). It uses the stratagem if that share is at least `kExplosivesMinShare`
  (0.05). The selection then names that enemy unit and its first legal model.
- **Crushing Impact:** in `decide_stratagems`, at the AfterChargeMove window, the bot takes the pair with the best
  expected gain. The gain is the enemy's share for Binomial(T, 1/3) less the unit's own for Binomial(T, 1/6), each
  capped at 6. It uses the stratagem if the gain is at least `kCrushMinGain` (0.05).
- **Fallback:** declines the window, takes the first legal pair at the selection, and ends the Shoot step as now.
- **Measured** by D-092's keep rule, 400 games each, on the terrain scenario (CSM Grenades against Tyranid MONSTERs),
  for `explosives`, `crushing_impact` and both. The defaults follow from the results.
  - The identity set (13 runs × 200 games, `--per-game 1`) against the M96 binary at the defaults: every `state` is
    the same, and `steps`/`actions` change only by the session's Crushing Impact declines.
  - p95 stays within budget.
  - Pinned bot games are re-pinned where declines are added (actions only).
- **Not in M97:** the charge planning doesn't price Crushing Impact; it is judged only at the window.
