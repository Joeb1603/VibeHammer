## D-090: The bot plays for cover: terrain in the move score, cover and hidden spots, detours, hand deployment (M89; uses D-087–D-089)
**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (M89's plan mode; design approved by the user, with three choices made there: the head-to-head
is 4 x 100 games, the M89 bot on each side with each side going first; `MoveWeights::cover` blends from 0 to 1; hand
deployment is in scope, the army plan is not). Bot and arena only: no rule, engine, state, action, event or file
format change. **Implemented:** M89 (see "In code" below). The head-to-head target (55%) is open: 54.3%.

**Why.** M88 gave the battlefield terrain, but the bot's move score rates shots on gaps alone: it never seeks cover,
never hides, and only the Shooting phase (through the engine) sees what terrain does to a shot.

**The gate.** `cover_on`: the battlefield has terrain and `MoveWeights::cover` is above 0 (default 1; the arena's
`--weights` key `cover`, 0 to 1). Each terrain-aware rate is `(1 - cover) x plain + cover x terrain`; a target that
can't be seen has a terrain rate of 0. With the gate off no terrain code runs: terrain-free play, and play at
`cover=0`, are the M88 bot's.

**The design** (PLAN.md, M89):
- `TerrainSight` (`src/ai/analysis/terrain_sight.*`) answers sight for hypothetical positions over one
  `core::TerrainView`'s blocker lists: area cover (13.08, first clause), could-hide (13.09, the record left to the
  caller), sees (13.09's 15", 13.10 / 13.11), unit sees (up to `kSightSamples` models a side), not fully visible
  (13.08, second clause). Model blockers (06.01) are ignored.
- `analysis::ShotContext::target_cover` worsens BS by 1 (D-089), `visible` false gives no volley.
- The enemy's reply: area cover at every end; the "stays" end with visibility and cover from the enemy's current
  models; an end where it moves sees again, except a hidden unit while the end gap is over 15". Hidden counts only when
  the move's plan makes no ranged attack this turn (13.09: our shooting is "the previous turn" in the enemy's turn).
- Our shots in the cheap score: a model that can't see the target adds nothing; the target's cover by area or by not
  being fully visible.
- Candidates after D-086's area spots: cover spots, hidden spots, a detour round a blocking polygon's corners.
- Hand deployment: M77's candidates sorted by the models with area cover, for a unit that takes cover.

**Open (left terrain-blind):** the army plan's group rates, threat tables, charges, fights, `ShootingAim::reply`, our
next turn's visibility, model blockers, holding fire on purpose to stay hidden, detours round circles.

**In code (M89).**
- **Sight** (`analysis/terrain_sight.*`, `TerrainSight`): masks for any pose (a box pre-test, then
  `model_within_area`), the view's per-mask blocker lists, and the 15" test for a hidden model. Lines are *estimated*:
  three parallel 1 mm lines between the two bases (the centres and near either side, 0.9 of the inscribed radius),
  each against the blockers whose box meets the pair's. Fully Visible is estimated by the target's two sides and its
  nearest point, each reached from one of the observer's three points past the cover blockers. Core's exact search
  (`has_line_of_sight` with terrain, `fully_visible_past`) took 70% of the bot's time and a p95 of 78 ms for the Chaos
  Space Marines. The estimate agrees with it on 3,985 of 4,000 random pairs within 24" on the terrain scenario (1,048 of
  them blocked) and on 2,936 of 2,952 for Fully Visible (`M89_Sight`). The engine still decides every real shot, and
  `refine_move_score`'s `best_shooting` refines the bot's best moves.
- **Samples:** `kSightSamples` = 3 models a side: the closest to the other unit's centroid, then the two extremes
  across the line between them.
- **The move score** (`move_score.*`):
  - `begin_reply` / `end_reply` hold our unit's view per plan;
  - `reply_rate` / `reply_wipe` blend the closest enemy unit's reply;
  - `other_threat` blends each other enemy unit's;
  - `our_rate` gives the enemy's area cover to our next turn's shots;
  - `our_shooting` / `our_shooting_at_other` give each model's sight and its target's cover.
  - The cache keys (`RateKey`, `Other::ranged`, the volley maps) carry the cover bit. `MoveScore::in_cover`, `hidden` and
    `unseen` are set for the reasons.
- **Candidates** (`movement.cpp`, `cover_spots_for`, `detours_for`), appended after every other candidate:
  - "into cover in <area>": a translation and a line abreast at the area's inner point;
  - "hides in <area>": a line abreast offset away from the closest enemy unit, every model within the area;
  - "behind <area>": past the far side of an obscuring area, in 1" steps until the unit stands;
  - "round the wall (left / right)": a blocking polygon's two silhouette corners (by angle), each pushed out along both
    edges' normals by the largest avoid radius + 0.3"; then leg 2 towards the enemy and, as a second candidate, on in
    the straight move's direction, each as far as it goes (bisected).
- **Hand deployment:** `heuristic_deploy_policy(..., cover)` and `deploy_cover_count`. With no enemy unit set up the
  candidates face the enemy zone's centre.
- **Arena:** the `cover` key (0 to 1) and the `cover (M89)` line, by target side.

- **Where the gain comes from:** almost all of it from our own shots (which enemies our models could see from where
  they end, and their targets' cover). Almost all of it goes to the Tyranids: the M89 bot on side B wins 151 of 200,
  against 140 for M88 against M88; on side A it wins 63 of 200, against 60.
- **What made no difference:** the reply's terrain and the candidates, each within the noise.
- **The defaults stay** (the user's choice, 2026-11-13). The 55% target is recorded as open: the final code wins 434
  of 800 (54.3%).
- **Corner ranking:** the detour's corners are ranked by a trig-free "diamond angle" (D-054's ban on trigonometry in
  the bot, checked by `M39_NoRulesInTheBot`), which ranks them as the angle does. Float rounding still changed 1 game
  in 40 on the terrain scenario against the first build, so every number below is the final code's.

**Final numbers** (the M89 binary, seeds 1–200; Chaos is side A; "M88" is the same binary at `cover=0`, which plays
exactly as the M88 bot):
- **Head-to-head, into cover** (seeds 101–200, the M89 bot's units as targets):
  - Facing the M88 Tyranids, the M89 Chaos Space Marines take more fire in cover: the Tyranids' selections into cover
    are 34.3% (A first) and 25.2% (B first), against 21.5% and 19.5% M88 against M88.
  - Facing the M88 Chaos Space Marines, the M89 Tyranids take less fire in cover (51.2% / 36.2% against 70.6% / 51.4%).
    They keep out of sight instead: the Chaos shooters are refused a Tyranid unit for Hidden 3.77 / 5.63 times a game,
    against 0.08 / 0.95.
- **M89 against M89, terrain scenario:** the Chaos Space Marines win 21.0% (A first) and 34.5% (B first), against
  25.0% / 38.5% M88 against M88. Their shots into cover fall to 58.5% / 37.8% (from 70.6% / 51.4%), because their
  models now pick targets they can see. The Tyranids are refused as Hidden 3.75 / 5.33 times a game (0.08 / 0.95).
- **Random armies with `--terrain`:** the Chaos Space Marines win 17.0% (auto) and 17.0% (hand), against 18.5% / 18.5%.
  Hidden refusals rise on both sides (auto: Tyranid units 2.04 a game against 0.34, Chaos units 1.54 against 0.17).
- **Decision time:** p95 28.6–28.7 ms for the Chaos Space Marines and 15.8–16.1 ms for the Tyranids on the terrain
  scenario (M88: 22.3 / 14.1 ms on this machine), 17.6 ms on random armies. Within the budget (twice M88's 16.6 ms).
- **Identity:** the nine runs of `docs/dev/bot_arena.md` at the default weights, and the four terrain runs at `cover=0`,
  are identical to the M88 binary (2,600 games, every `game` line).
- **Every run clean:** no rejected action, reverted move, fallback or unfinished game.

**Open** (PLAN.md, M89):
- **The 55% head-to-head:** 54.3%, below the target.
- **The army plan** (D-083 / D-086) still rates its groups' shots without terrain, so groups can form on targets that
  can't be seen.
- **Holding fire on purpose to stay hidden:** not done. Hidden counts only for a plan with no ranged attack this turn.
- **Charges round walls:** the charge odds ignore 13.06 detours (tried, and worse).
- **Also terrain-blind:** threat tables, charges, fights, `ShootingAim::reply` and our next turn's visibility.
- **Not counted:** model blockers; no detour is made round circular features.
