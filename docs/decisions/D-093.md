## D-093: The app-only rules audit: the scraped app text replaces the AI-sourced wording; re-targeting, Gone to Ground, control first at the end of a turn, the optional other weapon with [EXTRA ATTACKS], BS no worse than 6+ (M92; replaces D-012, amends D-009–D-011, D-015, D-069, D-071, D-084, D-089)
**Amended by D-097 (M96):** part 3's end-of-phase check also moved to entering the End of Phase step (before 15.08's and
15.11's windows), as the end-of-turn check did here.
**Decided:** 2026-11-23 (M92, the user). The 101 app-only entries in `docs/gdmissions/core_rules/` (gdmissions.app,
scraped 2026-11-20; third-party and not verified against the app) were compared with the working assumptions in D-001,
D-009, D-010, D-011, D-012, D-015 and D-069, and with every other decision that leans on an app-only rule. The user's
ruling: **do what the app text says, and let it override any wording they supplied before.** So where the scraped text
and an earlier decision differ, the scraped text wins, and the earlier wording is kept only where the two agree.

`docs/rules/` is still the source of truth for the PDF rules. The scraped app text is used only for the rules the PDF
leaves to the app. It is cited through this decision (`D-093: ...`), because the app's own numbers have three parts
(04.03.03) and every rule citation in the engine is `NN.NN` or a decision.

### What changed (the user's four answers, and one that follows from the ruling)

1. **04.03.03 Target No Longer Eligible or Viable (replaces D-012).** The app: "If a unit that was an eligible target for
   a rule or attack when it was selected stops being an eligible target (for example, because a rule enables it to make
   an out-of-phase move that takes it out of range), the controlling player can select new targets." D-012 said those
   attacks were lost and never moved to another unit.
   - *When:* a batch whose target is no longer on the battlefield (destroyed by an earlier batch of the same attacking
     unit; nothing else can make a target ineligible mid-attack yet). Read as "stops being an eligible target", so a
     destroyed target counts (the app's example is a move; a destroyed unit cannot be targeted, Appendix: Destroyed).
   - *The decision:* `decision::RetargetAttacks{attacker}` for the attacking unit's player, raised only when one or more
     of the lost batches' weapons has an eligible new target. "Can": the answer may select new targets for any of them,
     or none. `action::RetargetAttacks{retargets}` lists `{selection, target}` pairs (indices into the attack's weapon
     selections); every lost weapon not listed is lost, as before (`AttacksLost`, D-069: a random A lost unrolled).
     With no eligible new target the attacks are lost without asking, exactly as under D-012, so no replay changed.
   - *Eligible new targets:* shooting, the enemy units that the model could select with that weapon and profile now,
     with the unit's shooting type (`shot_problems`: range, visibility with terrain, engaged targets, 17.03, pistols,
     10.05 / 10.06), so `AttackFlow` records the shooting type (`shooting_type`, JSON omitted when Normal). Fighting,
     the enemy units the model is engaged with (04.02 While Fighting). A split melee weapon's selection keeps its
     declared attacks.
   - *The Select Targets facts are recorded again* for the new target (half range for [RAPID FIRE], model count for
     [BLAST], the 10.06 / 17.03 hit modifiers, the benefit of cover): the weapon is selecting a target now. The moved
     selections are gathered into new batches (04.03, D-006) appended after the old ones, so earlier batch indices stay;
     the old batches are done. `AttacksRetargeted{attacker, retargets, selections, first_batch, batches}`.
   - *Legal actions (D-004):* one answer per enemy unit that some lost weapon can target (every such weapon given that
     unit), then the empty answer. Any other combination of each weapon's options is valid too.
   - *What does not change:* 05.04's excess attacks within one batch, once every model of the target is destroyed, are
     still lost (PDF text). The [HAZARDOUS] count is from the Select Weapons step and stays.
   - *Bots:* the bot values each offered answer with the expected damage it already uses to order batches (the new
     batches and the attacks still to come against the same unit) and takes the best; none only when nothing is worth
     anything. The defaults and the fallback take the first offered answer.
   - *Client:* the action bar shows the engine's answers as buttons ("Retarget 2 weapons at Termagants (3)", "Select no
     new targets (the attacks are lost)"); the log and the dice cards follow the new batches. Choosing a different
     target per weapon is possible in the engine but not offered by the client (open).
2. **13.11.01 Gone to Ground (app-only, new).** "A model has gone to ground while all of the following apply: it is
   hidden; it is not fully visible to the attacking model due to one or more intervening dense terrain features; its
   unit did not make one or more ranged attacks during this turn or during the previous turn. While a model has gone to
   ground, subtract 3" from that model's detection range."
   - The third condition is already part of 13.09 Hidden (D-088), so: hidden, and not fully visible (D-088's Fully
     Visible) past the battlefield's Dense features alone. Its detection range is then 12".
   - Applied wherever 13.09's detection range is (`model_visible`, `visibility_problem`): every visibility check
     between an observer and a hidden model, the observer taking the place of "the attacking model".
   - The bot's sight estimate (`analysis/terrain_sight`) applies it too (one of its three lines crossing a Dense
     feature beyond 12"). `MoveScoreContext::moved_visible` still uses 15" (an estimate, open).
3. **14.02.01 Controlling an objective at the end of a phase or turn (app-only).** "At the end of each phase and turn,
   the first thing players do (before any other 'at the end of the phase/end of the turn' rules or abilities) is
   determine which objectives are controlled." So the end-of-turn check is made on entering the End of Turn step,
   before 03.03 Regaining Coherency's removals (D-084 had it after them, on leaving the step). The end-of-phase check
   was already the first thing at a phase's end.
4. **24.11.01 Only [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapons (app-only).** "If a unit has [Extra Attacks] weapons, it does not need to
   select weapons without the [Extra Attacks] ability in order to make attacks with those [Extra Attacks] weapons." So a
   model may select its [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapons alone even when it has another melee weapon it can use (D-071 refused
   that). It must still select all of its [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapons, and at most one other. The client's default plans
   and the bots still select the other weapon.
5. **02.02.01 Modifiers: BS no worse than 6+ (follows from the ruling; amends D-089).** "After all modifiers have been
   applied: ... BS cannot be 1+ (or better) or 7+ (or worse)." D-089 let the benefit of cover worsen a BS 6+ to 7+. Now
   it stops at 6+. No weapon in the data has a BS worse than 4+, so nothing plays differently today.

### What matched (no change)

- **D-001 / 04.01.03 Multiple Weapon Profiles:** per model. The app adds Hunter profiles ("can only target units with
  the specified keywords"); they stay refused (PLAN.md, Later, item 5).
- **D-009 / 01.04.01 Within / Wholly Within:** the same ("any distance that is not more than that distance"; any part of
  the base; every model for a unit).
- **D-010 / 01.04.02 Closest or Nearest:** the same, ties chosen by the controlling player of the unit using the rule.
- **D-011 / 02.02.01 Modifiers and 01.05.02 Re-rolls:** the same for dice rolls: re-rolls before modifiers, "unmodified"
  means after re-rolls and before modifiers, no maximum, a floor of 1, hit and wound rolls capped at +1/-1. The app adds:
  charge rolls cannot be more than 12 (no charge modifiers exist yet), a roll of several dice added together is re-rolled
  whole (no such re-roll exists yet), a re-rolled die is still a dice roll, the characteristic order (set, multiply, add,
  divide, subtract, round up) and floors. 01.05.01: an automatic success is not a critical hit or wound, as [TORRENT]
  already resolves (24.37).
- **D-015 / 08.03.01 Multiple Battle-shock Rolls:** the app's text is narrower (at most one roll per unit in the
  Battle-shock step, and a roll made there for another reason replaces the usual one), but no rule in the engine makes a
  second roll there, so nothing changes. D-015's wording is replaced by the app's.
- **D-069 / 02.02.03 Random Characteristics:** the same: A when the attacks are generated at 04.03, each weapon making
  identical attacks rolled on its own and grouped; D rolled when the opposing player has selected the model. The app
  adds that the dice of a D are a "damage roll" and the number after the operator is part of the characteristic, not a
  modifier (no damage modifiers exist yet).
- **D-020 / 12.04.01 fight-phase passing, 01.02.01 starting strength, 03.01.01 different M, 05.04.04 Destroyed:** the same
  as the Rules Appendix text the engine uses (03.01.01 names every move type whose maximum is M, the Appendix normal and
  advance moves; no unit in the data mixes M).
- **D-073 / 17.03.01:** an engaged unit is still not eligible to shoot at an engaged MONSTER/VEHICLE except by
  close-quarters shooting, as implemented.
- **D-088 / 13.09.01:** in the first turn "the previous turn" made no ranged attacks, as `ranged_attacks` starts.
- **D-091 / 01.04.03 As Close As Possible (objective):** a model already within range may move but must end within range,
  as implemented.
- **14.01.01 Objectives not within a terrain area** (a 40 mm marker, within range at 3"): the default terrain battlefield
  uses terrain objectives (14.01). The objective scenario without terrain keeps its own mission's circular areas (D-084),
  a house mission, not the marker rule (open, with the mission pack).
- The rest (transports, reserves, stratagems, attached units, aircraft, psychic, healing, revived models, tokens, table
  quarters, out-of-phase rules, ...) concern rules the engine does not have yet; they are for the milestones that add
  those rules.
