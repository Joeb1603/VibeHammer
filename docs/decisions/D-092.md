## D-092: The bot plays terrain and objectives in every phase: five features behind keys, all off by default (M91; follows D-086, D-090, D-091)
**Decided:** 2026-11-20 (M91's plan mode; design approved by the user, with the keep rule chosen there; the outcome, all
five features off by default and the 55% target recorded as open, chosen by the user after the measurements).
Bot and arena only: no rule, engine, state, action, event or file format change.

**Why.** D-090 left the army plan, the shooting aim's reply, charges and fight moves terrain-blind, and left holding
fire on purpose to stay hidden undone. D-086 and D-091 left the objectives out of charges, pile-ins and
consolidations: M90 took an Objective Consolidation whenever it got models within range, unweighed.

**The keys** (`normal::MoveWeights`, arena `--weights`). Each has a gate, and nothing behind it is computed while the gate
is off. At 0 each plays exactly as M90.

| Key | Range | Gate | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `plan_terrain` | 0–1, a blend | `cover_on` | the army plan's group rates with terrain |
| `aim_terrain` | 0–1, a blend | `cover_on` | `ShootingAim::reply` with cover and sight |
| `hold_fire` | 0 or 1 | `cover_on` | holding fire to stay hidden (13.09) |
| `objective_charge` | ≥ 0, x `objective` | a mission and `objective` > 0 | the objective terms in charges |
| `objective_fight` | ≥ 0, x `objective` | a mission and `objective` > 0 | the objective terms in pile-ins and consolidations |

The new keys are not part of `objectives_on`, so M87's `zero_objectives` regression and M86's VP-tally switch are as
before. `escape_stands`' guard weights copy them (with them at 0, M90's guard). **All five default to 0** (below).

**The design.**
1. **The army plan with terrain** (`army_plan(cache, state, side, const PlanTerrain*)`; `Planner::ranged`).
   - A shot's target that takes cover with every model within a terrain area where it stands has the benefit of
     cover (13.08), both ways: our volleys at the enemy and its reply on our units.
   - A shoot track that doesn't move (its gap within its longest range) shoots only if its models see the target from
     where they stand (13.09-13.11); a target not fully visible to it has cover too (D-089).
   - Each such rate is `(1 - w) x plain + w x terrain`. The terrain-blind cache is as before, and a second one keyed the
     same way holds the covered rates.
   - **The sight.** The new helpers `analysis::unit_view` and `area_cover_now` (`terrain_sight.*`) do one standing unit
     looking at another, as `MoveScoreContext::stays_view` judges the enemy's reply; the move score's own code is
     unchanged.
   - **One sight per decision.** The callers share one `TerrainSight`. `MoveScoreContext` takes the decision's (it
     builds its own as before when given none), and `decide_move_units`, the Shooting phase and the charge decisions
     build one for their plan.
2. **`ShootingAim::reply`** (with `ShootTerrain`): the enemy target's next-turn reply on each of our units blends in our
   unit's area cover where it stands and whether the target's sampled models see it now (our unit shoots this turn, so
   it isn't hidden in the enemy's).
3. **Holding fire to stay hidden** (13.09).
   - *The move score.* An end where the unit could hide (it takes cover, every model within an area with a Dense
     feature, `may_hold_fire`) and would shoot is also scored holding fire: no shot, and Hidden counts in the enemy's
     reply. The better is kept, flagged `MoveScore::holds_fire`.
   - *The Shooting phase.* `choose_shooting` scores each hidden unit's stay through `refine_move_score` (the engine's
     best shooting now), and leaves the unit out when holding fire scores better. "End shooting: <units> hold fire to
     stay hidden (13.09, M91)".
4. **Objectives in charges.**
   - `decide_charge` and `decide_charge_targets` take the weights and pass `ChargeObjectives` (the plan and `objective
     x objective_charge`) to `charge_prospect`, `charged_value` and `hold_value`.
   - Each adds `charge_objective_value`: D-086's outlook and terms at the unit's positions, after the planned charge move
     or where it stands, at the share of it left after the enemy's turn (`charged_parts` returns it from each of its
     three branches; `hold_value` from its exchange).
   - *Engaging a holder.* `objective_outlook` takes the enemy units' surviving shares: a charged target's OC counts at
     the share left after this Fight phase, rounded, both now and in its reach.
5. **Objectives in pile-ins and consolidations.**
   - `fight_outlook` covers both turns. In ours it is `objective_outlook` with no plan. In the enemy's, control at the
     end of this turn is what we score next, the enemy's level being what it has in range now (survival 1).
   - The gains of `decide_pile_in`, `decide_overrun_pile_in`, the Overrun pile-in inside `decide_fight_selection` and
     `decide_consolidation` add the moving unit's terms after the move minus before it ("+x for objectives").
   - Objective Consolidations are weighed with the others; M90's rule stays the tie-break.
6. **The arena** adds "charges onto an objective a game (M91)" (committed charge moves with a model within range of an
   area) and, with terrain, "holds of fire a game (M91)" (units that could shoot, every model hidden, when the bot ended
   a Shoot step). The `--per-game` line is unchanged.

**The keep rule** (the user's choice, 2026-11-20):
- Each feature is switched off alone against the full set, on the same 400 games: the terrain scenario, the M91 bot on
  side A and on side B, each with `--first A` and `--first B`, 100 games each, seeds 1–100.
- A feature keeps its default only if the full set wins at least 10 more games (one standard deviation) than the set
  without it.

**Measured** (Release arena, the M91 binary, against the M90 bot, which is the same binary with the five keys at 0; M90
against M90 wins 200 of 400):

| M91 variant | Wins of 400 | on A (A first + B first) | on B |
|---|---|---|---|
| the full set (all five at 1) | 201 | 19 + 33 | 82 + 67 |
| without `plan_terrain` | 205 | 22 + 37 | 79 + 67 |
| without `aim_terrain` | 201 | 19 + 34 | 80 + 68 |
| without `hold_fire` | 200 | 18 + 33 | 82 + 67 |
| without `objective_charge` | 198 | 19 + 32 | 82 + 65 |
| without `objective_fight` | 199 | 19 + 31 | 82 + 67 |
| `objective_charge` and `objective_fight` at 3 | 200 | 19 + 32 | 82 + 67 |
| the full set on the objective scenario | 201 | 33 + 32 | 68 + 68 |

- **What happened.** No feature moved the result, alone or together.
  - *Holding fire* never happened in these armies, 0 a game. The Chaos Space Marine units are hardly ever hidden, and
    the Tyranids have almost no guns that reach past 15", so there is no reply to avoid. `M91_HoldFire` shows it working
    on a fixture where a Hive Tyrant has its heavy venom cannon: one lascannon Havoc holds fire, the whole unit shoots.
  - *Charges onto an objective* happen 0.05–0.35 times a game on the terrain scenario and up to 0.9 for the Tyranids on
    the objective scenario.
  - *Objective Consolidations* happen about 0.1 a game.
  - *Fight moves* come mostly late and away from the areas.
- **Decision time.** p95 about 40 ms for the Chaos Space Marines and 25 ms for the Tyranids on the terrain scenario,
  with four runs at once. That is the same for the M91 and the M90 bot on side A, so the features add no measurable
  cost.

**The outcome** (the user's choice, 2026-11-20). By the keep rule none of the five is on by default. **All five keys
default to 0**, so the M91 bot at the defaults is the M90 bot, and every M90 reference stays the reference:
- the code stays behind its keys and is tested (`tests/ai/m91_test.cpp` switches them on);
- the m91 replay is played with them on;
- **the 55% head-to-head target is open:** 201 of 400 (50.2%).

**Identity** (`--per-game` diffs against the M90 binary, 200 games each; 2,600 of 2,600 games identical): V1, both bot scenarios, Strike, the rosters,
the objective scenario, the terrain scenario with A first and with B first, random armies (auto and hand, with and
without `--terrain`) and the M73 lists, all at the defaults; and the terrain and objective scenarios with the five keys
at 0 (`M91_Regression`, seed 1).

**Tests and replays.**
- `M91_ArmyPlan`, `M91_HoldFire`, `M91_Charge`, `M91_FightMoves`, `M91_Regression`, `M91_Arena` and `M91_Replay`.
- Pinned to the five keys at 0, as M87 and M89 did: `M89_Regression`, the M90 bot tests and the m87 and m89 replay
  generators. With the defaults at 0 these pins change nothing, but they keep those tests M90's if a default changes.
- New: `tests/replays/m91_bot_game.json`, the features on (see `docs/dev/replays.md`). Every older replay is
  byte-identical.

**Open.**
- The 55% target.
- Levers outside M91's scope: other armies (Tyranids with long-range guns would make holding fire matter), terrain
  layouts that put the areas where fights happen, and an asymmetric mission.
- Still terrain-blind: threat tables, the charge odds round walls (13.06), model blockers, and detours round circles.
