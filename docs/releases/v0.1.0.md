The first release of VibeHammer: a Warhammer 40,000 (11th edition) skirmish for Windows. A Space Marine
Intercessor Squad fights a Tyranid Termagant unit on a 24" x 24" board, following the core rules.

## How to play
1. Download `VibeHammer-v0.1.0-windows-x64.zip` below and unzip the whole folder. Don't run the exe from
   inside the zip.
2. Double-click `VibeHammer.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, click
   "More info" and then "Run anyway".
3. On the start screen, choose one of these:
   - **Play hotseat:** two players share the computer.
   - **Play vs bot:** you choose Space Marines or Tyranids, and the bot plays the other side.
   - **Watch bot vs bot:** the bot plays both sides.
   - **Continue saved game** or **Watch a replay**.

`README.txt` in the zip has more detail.

## What's in it
- **The full turn:** the Command, Movement, Shooting, Charge and Fight phases, with battle-shock, the
  attack sequence (hit, wound, save, damage), allocation groups, pile-in and consolidation, and five battle
  rounds.
- **Playing on the board:** drag models along their paths. The game shows rings for movement range,
  Engagement Range, coherency, weapon range and charge distance, shades the area each model can reach, can
  fix an illegal move for you, and has a ruler. Picking units, targets and casualties is done by clicking
  the board.
- **Dice pop-ups** for every roll, a dice log and an event log that cites rule numbers, and datasheet
  windows that explain weapon abilities and keywords.
- **Undo, save and load,** and a replay viewer. A few example games are included under `tests/replays`.
- **The bot** has three levels:
  - **Easy:** the baseline.
  - **Normal:** plans its shooting, charges, fights and moves from the odds.
  - **Hard:** weighs Normal's plans against its chances of winning and looks ahead.

  The bot plays at Slow, Normal, Fast or Instant speed. "Pause the bots" (key P) and Step (key N) let you
  follow it one action at a time. While the bots are paused, you roll the bot's dice yourself.
- **Settings:** the start screen's options are saved in `vibehammer_settings.json` next to the exe. Delete
  that file to go back to the defaults.

## Not in this version yet
Terrain, objectives and missions, stratagems, deployment, unit-specific abilities (such as Bolter
Discipline and Synapse), and any units besides these two.

## Build notes
This release was built and packaged by GitHub Actions on Windows, and the full test suite passed in
Release. The CI machine has no graphics card, so the exe itself was not launched there.
