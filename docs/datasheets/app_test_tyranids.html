Rules
Army Roster (Xenos - Tyranids)
Synapse
  • Restriction: Army Faction TYRANIDS
  • Result:
  • Synapse Range: a TYRANIDS unit of yours ≤6" from 1+ friendly SYNAPSE models is inside that model's Synapse Range and your army's Synapse Range too
  • inside your army's Synapse Range, a TYRANIDS unit of yours gets:
    • Battle‑shock tests on 3D6 rather than 2D6
    • +1 Strength characteristic on melee attacks by its models
Assault:

[ASSAULT] weapon on ≥1 model: its unit may use assault shooting.

Blast:
  • Restriction: [BLAST] weapons (or [BLAST X])
  • Timing: gathering attack dice for the weapon
  • Result:
  • +1 attack dice per full 5 models the target unit had in the Select Targets step
  • with [BLAST X]: +X attack dice per full 5 models instead
Deadly Demise 1 / Deadly Demise D3 / Deadly Demise D6:
  • Restriction: appears as Deadly Demise X
  • Timing: one of this unit’s models gets destroyed; occurs after any embarked units finished their emergency disembark moves
  • Result: roll 1D6; on 6 that model suffers a deadly demise
  • every unit within 6" of it takes X mortal wounds
  • if X is random, roll it once per unit
Extra Attacks:
  • Timing: Unit having ≥1 model with an [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapon fights
  • Result:
  • Those weapons attack alongside the models' other weapons
  • Select Weapons step, each such model must pick:
  • - every [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapon it has
  • - 1× other melee weapon, where available
Feel No Pain 5+:
  • Restriction: written Feel No Pain X+; carried by models
  • Timing: a wound is about to be lost by such a model
  • Result: 1D6 roll; X+ cancels the loss
Heavy:
  • Restriction: [HEAVY] weapons, Shooting phase of yours
  • Condition: three things at once: attacker is unengaged; it did not arrive via set-up this turn; No model in it has Moved beyond 3" this turn
  • Result: attack gets +1 on the hit roll
Hyper Adaptions:
  • Timing: opening of battle round 1
  • Select: 1 of 3 Hyper-adaptations, active for your Tyranids units
  • Result: a Tyranids model holding the chosen one gets:
  • SWARMING INSTINCTS: attacks vs enemy Infantry or Swarm unit gain [SUSTAINED HITS 1]
  • HYPER-AGGRESSION: attacks vs enemy Monster or Vehicle unit gain [LETHAL HITS]
  • HIVE PREDATORS: attacks vs enemy Character unit gain [PRECISION] when a Critical Hit is scored
  • Duration: rest of the battle
Leader:
  • Timing: Muster Armies step, pre-battle
  • Select: Each leader or support unit of yours picks 1× friendly bodyguard unit it can lead (optional)
  • Result:
  • It leads that bodyguard unit all battle; together they form an attached unit
  • Default cap per bodyguard unit: 1 leader unit + 1 support unit
Lethal Hits:
  • Restriction: [LETHAL HITS] weapons
  • Timing: an attack scores a critical hit
  • Result: you may make that attack wound its target automatically (optional)
Precision:
  • Restriction: attacks made with at least one [PRECISION] weapon
  • Timing: Allocation Order step of those attacks
  • Condition: target unit includes a CHARACTER model that is visible to an attacking model
  • Select: the active player may choose 1× allocation group holding such a visible CHARACTER model (optional)
  • Result: the chosen group takes over as current allocation group
  • Duration: ends at the earlier of: all those attacks resolved, or that CHARACTER group destroyed
Scouts 8":
  • Restriction: written Scouts X"; the unit qualifies only if each of its models has it
  • Timing: Resolve Pre-battle Abilities step
  • Select: 1 of 3 options by unit state (optional)
  • Result:
  • in strategic reserves: deploy it at any point of your deployment zone, wholly within it
  • deployment zone of yours, unit wholly within it: a scout move is allowed
  • embarked on a DEDICATED TRANSPORT standing wholly within that zone, all embarked models having Scouts: the DEDICATED TRANSPORT is allowed a scout move
Shadow in the Warp:
  • Restriction: Army Faction TYRANIDS
  • Limits: 1× per battle
  • Timing: Command phase of either player
  • Condition: at least one unit of yours bearing this ability is on the battlefield
  • Result: (optional) unleash the Shadow in the Warp: every enemy unit present takes a Battle‑shock test
  • For such a test, an enemy unit ≤6" from 1+ SYNAPSE units of yours takes −1
Sustained Hits:
  • Restriction: appears as [SUSTAINED HITS X], on weapons
  • Timing: an attack with a [SUSTAINED HITS] weapon scores a critical hit
  • Result: that attack scores X extra hits on its target, on top of the critical hit itself
Synapse:
  • Restriction: Army Faction TYRANIDS
  • Result:
  • Synapse Range: a TYRANIDS unit of yours ≤6" from 1+ friendly SYNAPSE models is inside that model's Synapse Range and your army's Synapse Range too
  • inside your army's Synapse Range, a TYRANIDS unit of yours gets:
    • Battle‑shock tests on 3D6 rather than 2D6
    • +1 Strength characteristic on melee attacks by its models
Twin-linked:
  • Restriction: weapons with [TWIN-LINKED]
  • Result: the wound roll of each attack with it may be re-rolled
Stratagems
Invasion Fleet
PREDATORY IMPERATIVE 1CP
Invasion Fleet – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Your turn - Command phase
LIMITS: You may not re-pick the Hyper-adaptation chosen when the first battle round opened.

TIMING: Command phase (your turn).

SELECT: Two TYRANIDS units of yours at most, both in Synapse Range of your army; or, alternatively, a single TYRANIDS unit of yours wherever it is.

RESULT: Pick a Hyper-adaptation; it becomes active for the targeted units in addition to anything else active for your army, lasting until your next Command phase starts.
ENDLESS SWARM 1CP
Invasion Fleet – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Your turn - Command phase
TIMING: Command phase (your turn).

SELECT: Two Endless Multitude units of yours at most, both in Synapse Range of your army; or, alternatively, a single ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit of yours wherever it is.

RESULT: Per targeted unit, destroyed models come back to the battlefield: D3+3 at most.
ADRENAL SURGE 2CP
Invasion Fleet – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn).

SELECT: Pick one of two groups: a pair of TYRANIDS units of yours (at most two) inside your army's Synapse Range, each eligible to fight, or alternatively a single other TYRANIDS unit of yours eligible to fight.

RESULT: For the rest of this phase, whenever a model of a chosen unit attacks, any unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is a Critical Hit.
DEATH FRENZY 1CP
Invasion Fleet – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn), when an enemy unit finishes picking its targets.

SELECT: A single TYRANIDS unit of yours that has been targeted by at least one attack from that enemy unit.

RESULT: For the rest of the phase, a model of the target unit that is destroyed before having fought: roll one D6, on 4+ it remains on the battlefield for now.
• Once the unit of the model that destroyed it has finished its attacks, the destroyed model may fight, then it is removed.
OVERRUN 1CP
Invasion Fleet – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn), directly before a TYRANIDS unit of yours Consolidates.

SELECT: The TYRANIDS unit about to Consolidate.

RESULT: For the rest of the phase, whenever the unit Consolidates: its models get +3" of movement, provided the move can finish with the unit in Engagement Range of an enemy unit.
• Unit in Synapse Range of your army with no enemy in Engagement Range: it may trade the Consolidation move for a Normal move of ≤6".
RAPID REGENERATION 1CP
Invasion Fleet – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Shooting or Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's), or your opponent's Shooting phase; trigger: an enemy unit finishes picking its targets.

SELECT: A single TYRANIDS unit of yours that has been targeted by at least one attack from that enemy unit.

RESULT: Feel No Pain 6+ for the models of the target unit, for the rest of the phase.
• Target unit in Synapse Range of your army: Feel No Pain 5+ replaces it.

CRUSHING IMPACT 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Your turn - Charge phase
TIMING: Charge phase (your turn), directly after a friendly MONSTER/VEHICLE unit finishes a charge move.

SELECT: The MONSTER/VEHICLE unit that charged.

RESULT: Steps, in sequence:
1. Choose an enemy unit the target unit is engaged with.
2. Choose a model of the target unit that is itself within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
3. Roll as many D6 as that model's T characteristic: each 1 deals 1 mortal wound to the target unit, each 5+ deals 1 mortal wound to the chosen enemy unit. Cap: 6 mortal wounds per unit.
INSANE BRAVERY 1CP
Core – Epic Deed Stratagem
Your turn - Command phase
LIMITS: This stratagem may be used just once per battle.

TIMING: Battle-shock step of your Command phase, ahead of a battle-shock roll you are about to make on behalf of a friendly unit.

SELECT: The unit concerned.

RESULT: Treat the battle-shock roll as passed, with no dice needed.
EXPLOSIVES 1CP
Core – Wargear Stratagem
Your turn - Shooting phase
TIMING: Shooting phase (your turn).

SELECT: A single unengaged EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES unit of yours, eligible to shoot, that has not advanced this turn.

RESULT: Three steps:
1. Model: any EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES model of the target unit.
2. Victim: an unengaged enemy unit that the model sees (visible), at ≤8".
3. Six D6 thrown at the victim: it takes 1 mortal wound (06.02) per 4+.
HEROIC INTERVENTION 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Charge phase
TIMING: Charge phase (opponent's turn), at its end.

SELECT: A single friendly unengaged unit with an enemy unit ≤12" away. VEHICLE units qualify only with CHARACTER/WALKER.

RESULT: The unit performs a charge (11.02), choosing one of two modes before its charge roll:
▪ Into the Fray (+1CP): a modified charge roll above 6 counts as 6; eligible charge targets = every enemy unit ≤6" away that is also within maximum distance.
▪ Leap to Defend: valid charge targets are limited to enemies that completed a charge move in this phase, within maximum distance.
COUNTER-OFFENSIVE 2CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (opponent's turn), Fight step; an enemy unit has just resolved its attacks.

SELECT: A single friendly unit; it must be eligible to fight.

RESULT: Gains Fights First through the phase; it has to be your following pick to fight (12.04).
RAPID INGRESS 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Movement phase
LIMITS: Cannot be played in the first battle round.

TIMING: Last step of your opponent's Movement phase.

SELECT: A single friendly unit sitting in strategic reserves, AIRCRAFT excluded.

RESULT: Perform an ingress move (20.04) with your unit, bringing it in from reserves onto the battlefield.
FIRE OVERWATCH 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Movement phase
TIMING: As your opponent's Movement phase ends.

SELECT: A single unit of yours, unengaged, which is not TITANIC.

RESULT: It fires as snap shooting.
SMOKESCREEN 1CP
Core – Wargear Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Shooting phase
TIMING: Opening of your opponent's Shooting phase.

SELECT: A single friendly SMOKE unit.

RESULT: For the rest of the phase, attacks aimed at your SMOKE unit, and attacks aimed at a unit that models of your SMOKE unit shield from the attacker so it stops being fully visible, are resolved with the benefit of cover for the target (13.08).
COMMAND RE-ROLL 1CP
Core – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Any phase
TIMING: Any phase, either player's turn, you have just rolled one of these on behalf of a unit or model on your side: Wound roll, Hit roll, Save roll, Damage roll, Hazard roll, Charge roll, Advance roll, or the roll giving a weapon its number of attacks.

SELECT: The unit or model that roll was for.

RESULT: Roll again and keep the new result.
• Dice rolled together: re-roll a single die of your choice.
• Charge roll: the whole roll is repeated.
EPIC CHALLENGE 1CP
Core – Epic Deed Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn), right after you pick a friendly CHARACTER unit as selected to fight.

SELECT: The CHARACTER unit you picked.

RESULT: In your unit, choose a CHARACTER model. Its close-combat weapons gain [PRECISION] for the rest of this phase.
