Rules
Dark Pacts:
  • Timing: when a unit that has this ability gets selected to shoot or fight
  • Condition: your Army Faction is Heretic Astartes
  • Result: it may make a Dark Pact (optional)
  • First a Leadership test, ahead of the Dark Pact's effects; fail: D3 mortal wounds on the unit
  • Then pick 1 ability for the unit's weapons to gain:
    • [LETHAL HITS]
    • [SUSTAINED HITS 1]
  • Duration: rest of that phase
Deep Strike:
  • Restriction: all models of this unit need it
  • Timing: this unit performs an ingress move
  • Result: unit may arrive at any spot beyond 8" (horizontal) of every enemy unit; the enemy deployment zone does not bar it (optional)
Hazardous:
  • Timing: a unit that was selected to shoot / selected to fight has finished resolving its attacks
  • Result: count the [HAZARDOUS] weapons you chose at the Select Weapons step; the unit makes that many hazard rolls
Leader:
  • Timing: Muster Armies step, pre-battle
  • Select: Each leader or support unit of yours picks 1× friendly bodyguard unit it can lead (optional)
  • Result:
  • It leads that bodyguard unit all battle; together they form an attached unit
  • Default cap per bodyguard unit: 1 leader unit + 1 support unit
Pistol:
  • Restriction: Weapon rule
  • Result:
  • [PISTOL] = [CLOSE-QUARTERS] for every purpose
  • Unit with ≥1 model with a [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapon: may use close-quarters shooting
  • Different shooting type: each model (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE) attacks with only one group:
  • - [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapons (≥1)
  • - remaining ranged weapons (≥1)
Raiders and Reavers:
  • Restriction: your Heretic Astartes models
  • Result:
  • Ranged weapons gain [ASSAULT]
  • Armour Penetration characteristic of an attack improves by 1 when the target stands in an objective marker's range
Rapid Fire:
  • Restriction: weapons only, written [RAPID FIRE X]
  • Timing: gathering attack dice for the [RAPID FIRE] weapon
  • Condition: at the Select Targets step the target was no farther than half the weapon's range
  • Result: X extra attack dice join the pool
Stratagems
Renegade Raiders
REAVERS' HASTE 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Your turn - Charge phase
TIMING: Charge phase (your turn).

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES INFANTRY or HERETIC ASTARTES MOUNTED unit of yours.

RESULT: Rest of the phase: the unit is eligible to declare charges though it Advanced. Add +1 to its Charge roll when any charge target is within range of an objective marker.
WARPCHARGED ENGINES 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Wargear Stratagem
Your turn - Movement phase
TIMING: Movement phase (your turn).

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES TRANSPORT or HERETIC ASTARTES MOUNTED unit of yours not yet selected to move.

RESULT: Rest of the phase: if it Advances, no Advance roll is made; its models gain +6" to the Move characteristic for the remainder of the phase.
RUINOUS RAID 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Your turn - Shooting or Fight phase
TIMING: Shooting or Fight phase (your turn).

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES unit of yours, not yet selected to shoot or fight, with a TRANSPORT it disembarked from earlier this turn.

RESULT: Rest of the phase: attacks by its models may re-roll both the Hit roll and the Wound roll whenever an objective marker has the attack's target within range.
SCOUR AND SEIZE 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn).

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES unit of yours not yet selected to fight.

RESULT: Rest of the phase: your models' attacks gain the [PRECISION] ability when their target is in range of any objective marker.
OPPORTUNISTIC RAIDERS 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase ends.

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES unit of yours that was eligible to fight.

RESULT: With no enemy in Engagement Range, a Normal move of up to 6" is allowed (12" if MOUNTED); otherwise a Fall Back move is. A unit that disembarked from a TRANSPORT this turn cannot embark at the move's end.
UNFAILINGLY OBDURATE 1CP
Renegade Raiders – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Shooting or Fight phase
TIMING: Opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase (either player's), immediately after an enemy unit finishes choosing targets.

SELECT: A single HERETIC ASTARTES unit of yours, not DAMNED, targeted by at least one attack from that enemy unit.

RESULT: Each attack aimed at your unit has its Armour Penetration characteristic worsened by 1, till that enemy unit has resolved all its attacks.

CRUSHING IMPACT 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Your turn - Charge phase
TIMING: Charge phase (your turn), directly after a friendly MONSTER/VEHICLE unit finishes a charge move.

SELECT: The MONSTER/VEHICLE unit that charged.

RESULT: Steps, in sequence:
1. Choose an enemy unit the target unit is engaged with.
2. Choose a model of the target unit that is itself within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
3. Roll as many D6 as that model's T characteristic: each 1 deals 1 mortal wound to the target unit, each 5+ deals 1 mortal wound to the chosen enemy unit. Cap: 6 mortal wounds per unit.
INSANE BRAVERY 1CP
Core – Epic Deed Stratagem
Your turn - Command phase
LIMITS: This stratagem may be used just once per battle.

TIMING: Battle-shock step of your Command phase, ahead of a battle-shock roll you are about to make on behalf of a friendly unit.

SELECT: The unit concerned.

RESULT: Treat the battle-shock roll as passed, with no dice needed.
EXPLOSIVES 1CP
Core – Wargear Stratagem
Your turn - Shooting phase
TIMING: Shooting phase (your turn).

SELECT: A single unengaged EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES unit of yours, eligible to shoot, that has not advanced this turn.

RESULT: Three steps:
1. Model: any EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES model of the target unit.
2. Victim: an unengaged enemy unit that the model sees (visible), at ≤8".
3. Six D6 thrown at the victim: it takes 1 mortal wound (06.02) per 4+.
HEROIC INTERVENTION 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Charge phase
TIMING: Charge phase (opponent's turn), at its end.

SELECT: A single friendly unengaged unit with an enemy unit ≤12" away. VEHICLE units qualify only with CHARACTER/WALKER.

RESULT: The unit performs a charge (11.02), choosing one of two modes before its charge roll:
▪ Into the Fray (+1CP): a modified charge roll above 6 counts as 6; eligible charge targets = every enemy unit ≤6" away that is also within maximum distance.
▪ Leap to Defend: valid charge targets are limited to enemies that completed a charge move in this phase, within maximum distance.
COUNTER-OFFENSIVE 2CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (opponent's turn), Fight step; an enemy unit has just resolved its attacks.

SELECT: A single friendly unit; it must be eligible to fight.

RESULT: Gains Fights First through the phase; it has to be your following pick to fight (12.04).
RAPID INGRESS 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Movement phase
LIMITS: Cannot be played in the first battle round.

TIMING: Last step of your opponent's Movement phase.

SELECT: A single friendly unit sitting in strategic reserves, AIRCRAFT excluded.

RESULT: Perform an ingress move (20.04) with your unit, bringing it in from reserves onto the battlefield.
FIRE OVERWATCH 1CP
Core – Strategic Ploy Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Movement phase
TIMING: As your opponent's Movement phase ends.

SELECT: A single unit of yours, unengaged, which is not TITANIC.

RESULT: It fires as snap shooting.
SMOKESCREEN 1CP
Core – Wargear Stratagem
Opponent's turn - Shooting phase
TIMING: Opening of your opponent's Shooting phase.

SELECT: A single friendly SMOKE unit.

RESULT: For the rest of the phase, attacks aimed at your SMOKE unit, and attacks aimed at a unit that models of your SMOKE unit shield from the attacker so it stops being fully visible, are resolved with the benefit of cover for the target (13.08).
COMMAND RE-ROLL 1CP
Core – Battle Tactic Stratagem
Either player's turn - Any phase
TIMING: Any phase, either player's turn, you have just rolled one of these on behalf of a unit or model on your side: Wound roll, Hit roll, Save roll, Damage roll, Hazard roll, Charge roll, Advance roll, or the roll giving a weapon its number of attacks.

SELECT: The unit or model that roll was for.

RESULT: Roll again and keep the new result.
• Dice rolled together: re-roll a single die of your choice.
• Charge roll: the whole roll is repeated.
EPIC CHALLENGE 1CP
Core – Epic Deed Stratagem
Either player's turn - Fight phase
TIMING: Fight phase (either player's turn), right after you pick a friendly CHARACTER unit as selected to fight.

SELECT: The CHARACTER unit you picked.

RESULT: In your unit, choose a CHARACTER model. Its close-combat weapons gain [PRECISION] for the rest of this phase.
